# App Review notes — 1.0.4 (build 68 candidate)

Reconciled on 13 October 2026 with the machine-uploaded
[review notes](metadata/en-US/review_notes.txt). The text below mirrors that file.
These are local candidate notes; upload, submission and approval are separate
states recorded by dated release receipts. Measurements and 3D tools remain
Coming soon and are not included in any plan. No new demo credentials are added.

Rendprop creates shareable listing pages from phone walkthroughs and photos, with photo editing, reels and client contact cards. Walkthrough output is the user's recorded video, not captured drone footage or a production 3D walkthrough.

ACCESS: Sign in with Apple creates or restores the account that owns listings, hosted pages, AI usage, Studio access and team membership. Use your own Apple ID. There is no password, guest skip or external purchase link. Terms, Privacy and help are on the entry screen. Delete account: Settings > Your data > Delete account.

BUILD 68: Reviewed photo selections are checked before reels. Reel jobs keep accepted requests and finished clips. Closing the screen can keep work running; iPhone may pause it. Reopen that listing’s Reel Studio to recover or continue. Saved clips can finish without generating again. The organized listing ZIP includes eligible files saved on this iPhone, retained masters and disclosure notes; it does not fetch cloud-only files.

SPATIAL: Home and each listing's toolbox show "Measurements", "3D floor plan" and "3D walkthrough" as Coming soon cards. They open nothing, are not sold and no plan includes them. No RoomPlan/LiDAR scanning, measurement entry or plan import is reachable in this build. The experimental photorealistic spatial walkthrough is not in this build.

REVIEW: After Sign in with Apple, Home > See it in action opens the hosted sample. Watch fly-through opens Explore; Play uses normal playback. To create: Home > Get started; if offered, choose Start a new home. Enter a name/address > Save and continue, then record or import a walkthrough, tag rooms and create the tour. Standard rendering is on-device. The first published listing is free; new accounts include 5 AI photo edits, subject to usage limits. Camera capture needs a physical device.

SUBSCRIPTIONS: StoreKit 2, three products in rendprop_plans: Starter, Pro and Team, monthly only. Settings > Plan & usage offers plan changes, Restore purchases and Manage subscription. Apple handles changes/cancellation. Restore/management remain available if products fail to load. Prices use Product.displayPrice. Purchases require the billing account’s owner/admin permission.

TRIAL: New accounts start free with one published listing and 5 AI photo edits. An eligible 7-day trial starts after Apple confirms the subscription and Rendprop verifies its signed transaction; otherwise the button says Subscribe. Price, renewal and cancellation are disclosed. Cancelling grants no access. An active Sandbox purchase during review can activate trial-level access. Trial AI has a limited allocation; the app reports budget/provider refusals.

DELETION: Settings > Your data > Delete account covers the account and its sole-member workspaces. Shared workspace content can remain for other members. Failures/pending cleanup are reported. Removing local data is separate; deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

AI: Consent names providers and media/text uses, including checks/fallback. Settings > Your data > AI processing revokes permission. Ask for anything clarifies a photo request before paid generation; repainting fixed finishes, remodeling and hiding damage are refused. Custom edits need comparison/approval before listing, cover, export or reels. Staging needs approval before listing, cover or reels. Pending staging masters are marked previews and have no platform copies in the coordinator ZIP. Comparison uses retained source copies; camera/library imports are JPEG copies and may contain prior edits. AI fidelity is not guaranteed. Altered media/exports carry disclosure.

USER CONTENT: shared listing/portfolio links and authorized team workspaces are supported. No public feed, comments or user chat. Publish only permitted content.
