The editable 1.0.3 App Information declaration was read and updated in App Store
Connect. Apple computed **12+** (`TWELVE_PLUS` in the API). The released 1.0.1
record was not edited. This is an observed result, not a requested rating.

Two answers changed from the historical launch declaration:

| Field | Current answer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User-generated content | Yes | Workspace members share listing media and projects; users publish their own content and receive listing inquiries. |
| Alcohol, tobacco or drug use or references | Infrequent | The restaurant/bar template includes Wine Bar, Full Bar and Happy Hour amenities. |

Every other answer was preserved and compared on API readback. The app has no
in-app browser for unrestricted web access, paid third-party ads, gambling or
contests. Subscription purchases use StoreKit. Assess any future content or
capability change before changing questionnaire answers; do not force 4+ by
answering No to features the app actually supports.

The September launch instructions and any old blanket “None/No to everything”
checklist are historical. Use this declaration and the dated release receipts
for 1.0.3, with Apple's current questionnaire definitions.
