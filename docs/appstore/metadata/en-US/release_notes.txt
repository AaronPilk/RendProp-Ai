First release.

Rendprop turns a walkthrough you film on your iPhone into a drone-style flythrough tour with a share link, and gives you the rest of the listing kit in the same app:

• Record a walkthrough, tag the rooms or areas, and get a tour people scroll through.
• Publish it as a branded page with your card and a contact form, plus a separate unbranded link for an MLS virtual-tour field.
• AI Photo Studio: blue sky, twilight sky, green lawn, tidy the room, add furniture — each edit labelled as virtually staged, with the original published beside it.
• Reels from your photos, with your own voiceover and word-by-word captions.
• AI aerial intro shot, always disclosed as AI-generated.
• Floor plans, scanned in 3D with RoomPlan on supported iPhones or uploaded.
• Modes for real estate, event venues, restaurants, retail stores, and gyms.
• Subscriptions with a 7-day free trial: Starter and Pro monthly or yearly, Team monthly.
