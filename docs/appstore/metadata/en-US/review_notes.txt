Rendprop turns a walkthrough filmed on an iPhone into a smooth "drone-style" property tour, hosted as a web link the user shares with clients.

NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED. This is what changed since the 5.1.1(v) rejection. Every feature — including publishing a tour, and including the subscription purchase — works on first launch with no registration of any kind. The app opens a session for itself in the background: no email, no phone, no password, nothing personal is asked for or stored. There is no sign-in screen anywhere in the normal flow.

NEW IN THIS BUILD (16): if the network is slow or briefly unavailable, the app now waits and retries on its own instead of falling back to the Sign in with Apple sheet. A dropped connection could previously make a feature look as though it required registration. It never did, and that sheet is now reachable only by choosing it in Settings.

Sign in with Apple is still offered, but only as an option, in Settings > Account. Its only purpose is to carry a workspace to a new device. That screen says so in those words and offers "Not now". It is never required to reach a feature and never required to buy.

THE ONE EXCEPTION, and nothing a reviewer needs is behind it: Settings > Team. A team seat belongs to a person, so starting a team or joining one with an invite code asks for Sign in with Apple. Everything else, including publishing a tour and buying a subscription, works with no account at all. Team seats are not sold inside this screen: the Team plan is an ordinary auto-renewable subscription bought through StoreKit like the other four, and there is no link anywhere in the app that leaves it to buy anything.

FREE WEEK: a new install gets seven days with a larger allowance (3 tours, 60 photo edits, 4 reel clips, 2 aerial intros), then drops to the free plan. It is automatic, needs no card and no account, and the last intro screen says so. It is deliberately NOT called a "free trial" in the UI, so it cannot be confused with the 7-day StoreKit introductory offer on the paid plans.

DEMO FLOW: Home > "See it in action" plays a live sample tour — scroll inside the video to move through the space. To build one: Home > "Add a home" > paste a listing link or type an address > "Upload a video" (any photo-library clip) or "Record a walkthrough" > tag rooms > "Create tour". The flythrough renders on the device.

SUBSCRIPTIONS: auto-renewable, StoreKit 2, five products in one group (rendprop_plans) — Starter and Pro monthly or yearly, Team monthly — each with a 7-day free introductory offer. They unlock monthly allowances for renders and AI (plan list in the description). Settings > "Plan & usage" > "Upgrade plan" reaches the paywall from a cold launch with no account; "Manage subscription" opens Apple's own sheet. Every price comes from StoreKit's Product.displayPrice, and there is no other way to pay inside the app. If the paywall says "Plans aren't available right now", StoreKit returned no products — that happens only before the subscriptions are approved alongside this build.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v)): Settings > "Your data" > "Delete account", always visible. It deletes the server-side account, the published tours, the uploaded media and the leads, then wipes local data. It works for a session that never signed in with Apple — the app creates the account silently, so it deletes it the same way.

AI-GENERATED CONTENT: the AI photo edits (sky, twilight, lawn, tidying, virtual staging) and the AI aerial intro produce altered imagery. Every altered asset carries an in-app disclosure, the tour page shows a persistent "Virtually staged" label, and the unaltered original is published alongside the edit. A listing's Compliance section lists every AI asset and exports the audit log.

USER CONTENT: users record their own spaces; the app says "Only record spaces you have the right to record and publish." There is no in-app feed and no way to browse another user's content.
