Rendprop helps agents prepare property photos, make listing and social videos, publish a property website, and manage buyer enquiries.

ACCESS: Use Sign in with Apple with your own Apple ID. There is no password or guest skip. An introduction can be viewed before sign-in. Terms, Privacy and help are on the entry screen. Delete account: Account > Settings & plan > Your data > Delete account.

REVIEW PATH: After sign-in, Home > See it in action opens a hosted sample. To make your own listing, use Home > Get started, enter the address and details, then add photos. Photos and optional edits can be reviewed before use. Publish the property website with a selected landscape Listing video or a recorded/uploaded walkthrough; the recorded walkthrough is optional when a Listing video is selected. The Listing video is the website's hero video. Open the listing > Website design to see Classic and the luxury-design previews. The luxury purchase is currently unavailable; Classic is the included design. Camera capture needs a physical device.

VIDEO AND AERIAL: Open a listing > Make a video to select photos, choose landscape Listing or portrait Social media, review framing and optional audio, then review the finished video. Photo Motion moves within the selected photos; it is not captured drone footage or a 3D walkthrough. Open the listing > Aerial intro to choose an original front exterior photo for an optional AI-generated opening. The user reviews that clip before choosing whether to use it at the beginning of a video. The new opening route is available to eligible paid accounts with aerial allowance and explicit provider permission; it is separate from the older private-beta aerial endpoint. Saved clips remain accessible and disclosed. Existing saved requests and finished clips can be recovered from their listings. Activity groups active, ready, attention-needed and historical work.

NOT IN THIS BUILD: Floor plans, measurements and 3D tours are Coming soon cards in the standard app. No RoomPlan/LiDAR or experimental spatial capture is reachable. These features are not sold in any plan.

SUBSCRIPTIONS: StoreKit 2 offers four monthly products in rendprop_plans: Pro $29.99/60 credits, Max $49.99/120, Ultra $99.99/250 and Team $249.99/600 with three seats (U.S. prices). The app displays Apple's localized Product.displayPrice. Annual and Team 4-9-seat products are not offered. An eligible 7-day Apple trial permits one draft listing and limited AI photo edits; video generation, aerial generation and website publication require the first paid renewal. Full paid allowances begin after the trial. Account > Settings & plan > Plan & usage includes Restore purchases and Manage subscription, available even if products fail to load. Billing belongs to the owning workspace; Apple manages changes and cancellation. Usage and budget refusals are shown in the app.

AI AND PRIVACY: Consent identifies providers and processing uses before selected media is sent. Account > Settings & plan > Your data > AI processing can revoke permission. Staging and custom edits require review before use. AI fidelity is not guaranteed; altered media and exports carry disclosure. Ask AI uses current screen context and text, not a screenshot. Only publish content you are allowed to share. There is no public feed, comments or user chat.

FILES AND DELETION: Share everything packages eligible files saved on this iPhone, retained masters and disclosure notes; it does not retrieve cloud-only files. Delete account removes the identity and sole-member workspaces; shared content can remain for other members. Clearing local data is separate. Deleting the app or account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
