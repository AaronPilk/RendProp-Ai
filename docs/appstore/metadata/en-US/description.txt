Create content. Share the listing.

Record or import a walkthrough, prepare your photos and make a reel on your iPhone. Bring them together on one listing page you can share by text, email or a printed QR code.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Walk the space and record on your iPhone.
2. Tag the rooms or areas you want people to jump to.
3. Choose your cover photo, review the content and publish.
4. Share the link. Visitors see photos and details first, then choose a scroll-through tour or ordinary video playback. Enquiries land in your Leads inbox.

TOOLS FOR YOUR LISTING
• AI Photo Studio — blue sky, twilight, lawn, decluttering, virtual staging and custom edits. Review the result, keep photo versions and export images with the disclosure for the changes you made. Compare to saved untouched originals.
• Reels — turn your photos into a short vertical video with your own voiceover and word-by-word captions.
• Aerial intro — an optional AI-generated opening clip based on your exterior photo, labelled as AI-generated. It is simulated imagery, not drone footage.
• Measurements and floor plans — enter wall lengths, draw room outlines or import a plan. Apple RoomPlan scanning needs a supported LiDAR iPhone. Verify dimensions before use.
• Listing links — a contact page for sharing and a separate unbranded tour link. Check your MLS's rules before posting.
• Client delivery — photographers and videographers can put their client's name, business, photo and contact details on each listing. Route enquiries to that client's email, track delivery in Leads and send them again when needed. The client does not need a Rendprop account.

NOT ONLY HOMES
Choose real estate, event venues, restaurants, retail stores or gyms. The listing fields, screen labels and samples match your business.

WORK ON YOUR PHONE OR IN STUDIO
Sign in with Apple to create your account. Your listings stay with your account across devices and Studio. Team agents keep their own listings; only the Team owner can switch between linked agents. Recording and editing work offline once you are signed in; publishing and cloud tools need an internet connection.
