Rendprop helps real estate agents prepare property photos, make videos and share a mobile property website. Start with your address and listing details, then add photos in the order you want buyers to see them.

PHOTOS THAT STAY ORGANIZED
Keep originals and saved edits separate. Review photo versions, compare changes, move between photos and choose what appears on your listing. Portrait and landscape guidance helps you choose the right images for your destination.

AI PHOTO STUDIO
Prepare selected photos with sky, lawn, twilight, decluttering, virtual staging and custom edits. Review every result before using it. AI can change details, and edited media carries disclosure.

MAKE A VIDEO
Choose Listing for a landscape video or Social media for a portrait video. Select your photos in order and review framing. Optional narration and music stay under your control. Review the finished result before saving, sharing or choosing it for your website.

YOUR PROPERTY WEBSITE
Bring together photos, listing details, contact information and your selected listing video. A recorded drone-style walkthrough is optional and separate. Room tags require a recorded or uploaded walkthrough. Share the website link with buyers and review enquiries in Leads. Classic is included with paid access. Signature Estate and Coastal Luxe have design samples; live property previews require a published website.

WORK ON IPHONE AND IN STUDIO
Sign in with Apple or sign in to an existing Rendprop account with email and password. Your account and listing records work across iPhone and Rendprop Studio. Local media is not automatically copied to every device. Internet is required for AI processing, publishing and Studio.

YOUR CONTENT, YOUR CHOICES
Cloud AI asks permission before sending selected content to named providers. You control what to review and publish. Only use content you have permission to share.
