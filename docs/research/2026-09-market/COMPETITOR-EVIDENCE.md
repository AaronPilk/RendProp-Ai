# Rendprop competitive evidence and product implications

## Decision

Rendprop should not position itself as the first product combining video and 3D. Giraffe360 explicitly markets cinematic walkthroughs, orbitals, reels and virtual-drone flythroughs generated from the digital twin underlying its tours and floor plans. Matterport sells photos, floor plans, video and a 3D tour together. CubiCasa offers video rendered from its 3D floor plans. These are materially different implementations, but they invalidate the broad exclusivity claim. [Giraffe360 video](https://www.giraffe360.com/us/the-product/video/), [Matterport Marketing Cloud](https://matterport.com/matterport-marketing-cloud), [CubiCasa packages](https://www.cubi.casa/pricing/).

The defensible hypothesis is narrower: a dependable phone-first workflow combining authentic agent narration, attractive video, inspectable captured rooms, privacy review and portable publishing. That hypothesis needs comparative field evidence; it is not established by this research.

Scope: eight competing businesses across solo agents, teams, photographers, brokerages and enterprise platforms. Sources were accessed September 10, 2026. **High confidence** means an explicit current official statement, not independently tested product quality. **Medium confidence** marks incomplete or indexed-only evidence. Missing public information means unknown, not absent. Prices retain their published currency and conditions; none is a negotiated quote.

## Eight competitors

### 1. Matterport: capture infrastructure plus a marketing platform

Matterport supports phone/tablet/360-camera capture on its entry offering; higher plans support its full camera ecosystem. Its current plans scale by active spaces and users: Free has one space/two users, Starter 5–20/three, Professional 20–150/ten, and Business 100–300/50. Floor plans and technical deliverables can cost extra. Paid dollar amounts did not render in the public page, so a current subscription price is **unverified**. Starter advertises embedding and distribution to Homes.com, Apartments.com and LoopNet. [Plans](https://matterport.com/plans).

Separately, Marketing Cloud advertises Digital Pro packages starting at **$219**, combining photos, a floor plan, video and a 3D tour, with media organization, MLS import and buyer analytics. This is a service/package starting price, not an unlimited SaaS price or proof of identical cinematic quality. **Confidence: high** on advertised scope. Rendprop competes with both capture software and a buyer who can outsource the whole job. [Marketing Cloud](https://matterport.com/matterport-marketing-cloud).

### 2. Zillow 3D Home / Showcase: a free capture product with distribution leverage

The FAQ explicitly permits mobile-only panorama tours; compatible 360 cameras are required for interactive floor plans and tablet capture. Its current compatibility section specifies iOS 18+ and listed Ricoh/Insta360 devices. Tours have no per-home, service or hosting fee. Zillow provides an unbranded MLS link and states for-sale tour/floor-plan syndication to Zillow, Trulia, Redfin and Realtor. External panoramas cannot be imported. These are linked-panorama tours, not evidence of unrestricted photorealistic 3D movement. [3D Home FAQ](https://www.zillow.com/3d-home/faq/).

Showcase adds prioritized search/map exposure, agent/team branding and email distribution. Its current public product page does not establish a universal dollar price. Zillow Pro's April 2026 terms describe seat-based subscriptions and separately priced Showcase purchases; do not conflate free 3D Home with free Showcase. **Confidence: high** on this distinction. Rendprop cannot reproduce portal placement merely by exporting better media. [Showcase](https://www.zillow.com/pro/showcase/), [Zillow Pro terms](https://www.zillow.com/pro/zillow-pro-product-terms/).

### 3. CubiCasa: phone capture, floor plans and a broader output ladder

CubiCasa lists iPhone XR and newer with iOS 18+, plus Android 12/ARCore devices. LiDAR is not mandatory; the vendor claims better accuracy with it. Scanning can proceed offline after initial online startup. [Supported devices](https://help.cubi.casa/en/articles/6661525-list-of-supported-hardware-devices).

LITE/Standard/PLUS/PLUS 3D progress from 2D plans through branding, reports and furnished 3D plans. A paid PLUS 3D add-on delivers a **1–3-minute MP4 walkthrough without audio**. This is a rendered floor-plan visualization, not demonstrated photographic reconstruction. The pricing page advertises Realtor.com/Redfin distribution but leaves locale-dependent numeric prices unresolved in its public text; no universal free tier or price is assumed. [Packages](https://www.cubi.casa/pricing/).

CubiCasa Tour positions uploaded property photos on a PLUS/PLUS 3D plan; creation is web-portal-only, with 30 MB/file and 700 MB total limits. Billing is per order without subscriptions. **Confidence: high.** Phone-only and floor-plan-linked media are already competitive territory; Rendprop's advantage must concern capture reliability, output quality or reduced workflow friction. [Tour requirements](https://help.cubi.casa/en/articles/9915464-how-to-create-a-cubicasa-tour), [Billing](https://help.cubi.casa/en/articles/13399058-how-does-cubicasa-s-pricing-and-billing-work).

### 4. iGUIDE: measured deliverables and ownership-oriented pricing

iGUIDE's PLANIX R1 combines dedicated capture hardware with a connected smart device. It advertises 3D tours, LiDAR measurements and floor plans, with DWG/RVT/ESX deliverables available through its workflow. The accuracy claims are vendor claims, not an independent comparison against Rendprop. [Hardware](https://goiguide.com/camera-hardware).

Processing charges depend on billable area; an operator's capture fee is separate. The public pricing page currently mixes currencies: Standard starts at **CAD 48**, Premium at **CAD 66**, while Instant is **USD 7.99/project** and lacks downloadable floor plans. These must not be combined into a fabricated USD comparison. Hosting is included, with no fee currently charged after the first year; processed tours can be downloaded for offline viewing/self-hosting. Hardware purchase is additional and its current price was not verified. **Confidence: high** on these published terms. Portable tours and pay-per-project billing are not novel promises Rendprop can claim exclusively. [Pricing](https://goiguide.com/pricing).

### 5. Giraffe360: the closest direct challenge to the combined-media thesis

The current US video page describes a Giraffe PRO camera capture feeding a digital twin, then reels, orbitals, virtual-drone flythroughs and room-to-room cinematic videos. It advertises voiceovers/captions, agent end cards and adjustable scene order, with MLS/social outputs. This is direct product overlap, not merely embedding someone else's video. **Confidence: high** for the advertised offering; a subsequent direct retrieval succeeded after the initial browser challenge. [Video](https://www.giraffe360.com/us/the-product/video/).

US/Canada PRO terms dated August 25, 2026 describe camera-plus-dashboard subscriptions, fair-use limits specified in the order/dashboard, and possible AI-enhancement, merge and integration fees. They assign created-content IP to the client while granting service-improvement/product-development rights that include AI training. Ownership therefore does not itself imply no training use. Public dollar pricing was not independently resolved; do **not** repeat the third-party $360/month claim as verified. **Confidence: medium:** detailed official indexed text was available, but direct page retrieval encountered a browser challenge. Confirm current entitlements and exit/hosting terms before a procurement comparison. [PRO terms](https://www.giraffe360.com/us/pro-camera-terms-of-service/).

### 6. Aryeo: operating system for media businesses, not a scanner substitute

Aryeo publishes **$49/month** for 100 annual listings/one team member; **$99** for 250/three; **$179** for 500/five; enterprise is custom, with 500+ listings and unlimited team members. Annual listing allowances are not monthly quotas. Its comparison names white-labeling, property websites, video hosting, scheduling, payments and interactive floor plans, although its malformed text table prevents dependable feature-to-tier mapping. Confirm specific entitlements before quoting them. [Pro pricing](https://www.aryeo.com/pro-pricing).

Public API documentation covers listings, videos, orders, appointments, scheduling, users and webhook setup. Camera requirements are not established by these sources: this is primarily a workflow/distribution competitor for Rendprop, and potentially an integration channel. **Confidence: high** on pricing cards and documented API surface; medium on tier mappings. Replacing photographers' existing order/payment operations would be a much larger scope than exporting media into them. [API reference](https://docs.aryeo.com/).

### 7. HomeJab: the alternative is often a service, not another app

HomeJab advertises nationwide real-estate media services starting at **$179** and a staffed capture workflow. Its enterprise offering names centralized dashboards, role-based access and consolidated billing for organizations such as brokerages and property managers. Those are advertised features, not audited controls. [HomeJab](https://homejab.com/).

Its live package builder combines photos, cinematic walkthroughs, aerial media, Matterport-powered 3D tours and floor plans. The final bundle total does not appear without configuration, and selected high-cost US markets incur address-dependent surcharges. The page lists virtual staging and virtual dusk at **$25/photo**. Older indexed flat-package tables should not override this current configurator. **Confidence: high.** The buyer is paying to avoid travel, scanning, editing and coordination; Rendprop must compare total human effort, not just software spend. No requirement that the agent own specialized capture hardware is established for this outsourced service. [Pricing](https://homejab.com/pricing/).

### 8. Realsee: multiple capture tiers plus a developer platform

Realsee's official platform lists phone, compatible 360-camera/gimbal and professional Galois/Poincaré capture routes. Its pricing guide distinguishes free generation for professional devices from lightweight generation that immediately deducts a credit covering the first 30 days of hosting. Active public hosting costs **one credit/project/month**; sample packs imply approximately **$2.97** or **$2.65/credit**, with one-year credit validity. Live guided co-viewing is ten credits/project/month; manual floor plans are quote-based. Export add-ons and hardware are separate. These are published guide examples, not a confirmed checkout quote. [Software pricing](https://www.realsee.ai/blogs/help-center/software-services-pricing).

Five SDK renders captured spaces in the browser; OpenAPI connects spatial workflows; the developer platform also advertises guided experiences and reconstruction capabilities. Production API pricing, regional hosting, SSO and contractual rights were not verified. **Confidence: high** on documented scope, lower on whole-life costs. A web spatial viewer/API is established competition, not a Rendprop-exclusive architecture. [Developer platform](https://developer.realsee.ai/).

## Segmentation changes the buying criterion

| Buyer | Competitive pressure | Rendprop proof required |
|---|---|---|
| Solo agent | Free Zillow tours; per-order CubiCasa; outsourced shoots | Finish a usable listing with an existing phone, minimal retries and no surprise costs |
| Small team | Shared seats, brand consistency, photographer handoffs | Explicit ownership, delegated work, reliable resumable uploads and shared review |
| Brokerage / media business | Aryeo workflows; HomeJab operations; Giraffe media bundles | Bulk listing operations, approvals, branded/unbranded output and dependable integration |
| Enterprise / platform | Matterport procurement controls; Realsee SDK/API | Tenant isolation, identity lifecycle, audit evidence, contractual rights and predictable service levels |

These are strategic inferences from the documented offerings, not claims that every buyer requests every feature. One product can serve all four, but a single undifferentiated roadmap will hide incompatible economics and support expectations.

Matterport Enterprise already documents SAML SSO, custom roles, API/SDK access and audit logs. Its Trust Center describes annual SOC 2 Type II audits, incident response, a DPA, subprocessors and security documentation. This is a concrete procurement benchmark—not a claim that certification guarantees safety or that every competitor has equivalent controls. [Enterprise plan](https://matterport.com/plans), [Trust Center](https://matterport.com/trust).

For Rendprop, the enterprise package should include a truthful security dossier, scoped service accounts, audit exports, role/seat lifecycle, data-retention/deletion evidence, export contracts and incident ownership. Negotiate jurisdiction/residency and service levels only after the implementation supports them. White-label branding, content ownership, portability and AI-training permission are separate requirements, not synonyms.

## Economics: a room's GPU bill is not its delivery cost

The brief's cents-per-room thesis remains unproved. iGUIDE itself distinguishes processing from capture labor. Realsee separately monetizes active hosting and deliverables. HomeJab's offer includes people. These are three different cost models, none directly comparable with one successful training run. [iGUIDE pricing](https://goiguide.com/pricing), [Realsee pricing](https://www.realsee.ai/blogs/help-center/software-services-pricing), [HomeJab](https://homejab.com/).

Measure contribution margin per **successfully delivered listing**, including failed scans, rejected reconstructions, retries, storage duration, preview processing, uploads, bandwidth, privacy review, support and refunds. Record agent minutes separately from provider dollars. Report median and tail latency/cost, not only the best room. A device-independent viewer still requires device-specific memory and frame-rate testing.

The validation set should deliberately include blank walls, mirrors, windows, clutter, stairs, dim rooms and non-LiDAR phones. Failures must remain in the denominator. A faithful-looking reconstruction can still contain holes or geometric errors; neither attractive splats nor LiDAR alone establish measurement-grade accuracy. Keep marketing visualization separate from area/valuation deliverables until measured against ground truth.

## Recommended engineering consequences

First, make one real listing demonstrably better end-to-end: recorded narration, stable room identities, durable capture recovery, explicit privacy approval, portable branded/unbranded outputs, and late addition of spatial rooms without rerendering unrelated video. Treat the last capability as a hypothesis to compare, not a verified competitor gap.

Second, make trust visible. Zillow already provides automatic face blur plus manual adjustments and expressly requires pre-publication review. Rendprop should prove that edits survive every derived asset and that unpublished raw captures cannot leak through public links. Privacy is necessary parity, not evidence of a moat. [Zillow FAQ](https://www.zillow.com/3d-home/faq/).

Third, design integration seams before rebuilding media-business software: stable listing/room IDs, versioned manifests, signed exports, idempotent job requests, explicit readiness/failure states and scoped callbacks. Aryeo/Realsee documentation demonstrates that platforms compete through these interfaces. Their existence does not authorize use or establish favorable API licensing.

Finally, require representative customer trials and negotiated enterprise diligence before promising market leadership, measurement accuracy or an all-inclusive price. Current evidence supports a serious market with strong incumbents—and a possible execution advantage—not uncontested territory.
