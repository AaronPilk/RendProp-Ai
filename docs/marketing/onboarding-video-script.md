# Rendprop — onboarding video script ("How it works")

A narrated 2½-minute walkthrough of the real app, recorded from the iOS
simulator. Not an App Store *App Preview* (those have their own rules) — a plain
MP4 for rendprop.com/support, social, and the first-run "Watch the sample tour"
slot later.

**Voice:** the app's own — plain, calm, human. No hype words, no "revolutionary".
One idea per segment. Say what the screen shows.

**Format of each segment** (the build tool `tools/video/build_onboarding.py`
parses these headings and the `Caption:` lines — keep the shape):

```
## NN · M:SS–M:SS · Title
On screen: what the tour does (the UI action OnboardingTour.swift performs)
Say: the spoken line(s)            ≤ 28 words per 8 seconds
Caption: the burned-in caption     shorter; 2 lines max on a phone frame
```

The times are **targets**. The real cut points come from the `TOUR_MARK` lines
the test writes while it runs (one per segment), so a segment that runs a second
long or short is fine — the narration and captions follow the marks. If a
narration file is longer than its segment, the build freezes that segment's last
frame until the narration ends.

Total: ~145 s of app footage + a 1.5 s title card + a 2 s end card ≈ 148 s.

---

## 00 · Title card (1.5 s, generated by the build)

On screen: "Rendprop" · "How it works" on a dark card. No narration.

---

## 01 · 0:00–0:08 · Home

On screen: Cold launch on Home in real-estate mode. The hero reads "Win the
listing. Skip the film crew." A slow scroll down to the "Make something" tiles
and back up.

Say: This is Rendprop. Walk through a space with your phone, and it becomes a
cinematic scroll-to-fly tour with a link you can send.

Caption: One walkthrough → a scroll-to-fly tour, plus a link

## 02 · 0:08–0:20 · Six industries

On screen: Tap the "Real estate" capsule at the top left. The menu lists all six
business types. Pick "Event venue" — Home re-themes to "Book the date before
they ever visit." Open the menu again and switch back to "Real estate".

Say: It isn't only for homes. Pick your business type up here — real estate,
venues, restaurants and bars, retail, gyms, or any other space — and the whole
app changes to match.

Caption: Six modes: real estate · venues · restaurants · retail · gyms · other

## 03 · 0:20–0:32 · Add a home

On screen: Tap "Add a home". On the New Home form, type "24 Willow Bend Court"
into the address field, put the keyboard away, and rest on "Step 2 · The video".

Say: Start with the space. Give it an address, or just a name. Everything you
make — photos, the tour, reels — is saved to it.

Caption: Add the home first — everything you make is saved to it

## 04 · 0:32–0:44 · Record or upload

On screen: Tap "Upload a video". The "Where is your video?" sheet offers Photos
or Files. Choose Photos, pick the walkthrough clip, and it imports straight into
Review & Submit. ("Record a walkthrough" is the other button — the in-app
camera can't run in the simulator, so it is named, not opened.)

Say: Then add the walkthrough. Record it in the app — it coaches your pace as
you walk — or upload a clip you already have.

Caption: Record in the app, or upload a clip you already have

## 05 · 0:44–0:56 · Tag the rooms

On screen: Tap "Tag rooms on the video". In the tagger, tap "Entry" at the start,
scrub forward, tap "Kitchen", then Done. Review & Submit now lists the two rooms
with their timestamps.

Say: Tag the rooms — or areas — as the walk goes past them. On the finished tour,
people tap a dot and jump straight there.

Caption: Tag rooms → tap-to-jump chapters on the tour

## 06 · 0:56–1:04 · Create the tour

On screen: Scroll to "Pick your quality" and the "Create my tour" button. Rest
there. (The button is not tapped in the standard recording — a render takes a
minute in the simulator and would blow the 150-second budget. Set
`TOUR_RENDER=1` on the bridge to tap it and wait.)

Say: Pick a quality and tap Create my tour. Rendprop renders the flythrough right
on your phone.

Caption: Create my tour — rendered on your phone

## 07 · 1:04–1:16 · The tour

On screen: Back on Home, tap "Watch the sample tour". The hosted demo listing
page opens — the page a shared link opens. Drag slowly inside it: the video
flies through the house as you scroll.

Say: This is what people get. They scroll, and they fly through the space. One
link, with the tour, your card, and a contact form on the same page.

Caption: Scroll to fly through · one link: tour, your card, contact form

## 08 · 1:16–1:30 · AI Photo Studio

On screen: Home → "Take photos" → the AI Photo Studio for the home. The one-tap
edits are listed: Make it twilight, Make the sky blue, Make the lawn green, Tidy
the room, Add furniture, Turn it into video. Two photos are added from the
library; the grid shows a wand on each and the line "Every AI edit is disclosed
on your tour, and the untouched original is published with it." No edit is run.

Say: The AI Photo Studio fixes listing photos in one tap: a blue sky, twilight, a
green lawn, a tidied room, or added furniture. Every AI edit is labelled on your
tour, and the untouched original is published beside it.

Caption: One-tap AI edits · every edit labelled, original published

## 09 · 1:30–1:42 · Reels with your own voice

On screen: Tap the "Make a reel" card. In Reel Studio, scroll to "Step 2 · Add
your voice" and tap "My voice" — the big Record button and "Your words become
captions on the video." Close without recording.

Say: Turn a few photos into a short vertical reel. Record your own voiceover, and
your words become captions on the video.

Caption: Reels — your own voiceover becomes the captions

## 10 · 1:42–1:56 · Aerial intro and floor plan

On screen: Open the home from Home. In its toolbox tap "Aerial intro" — the sheet
shows the shot settings and, above them, the always-visible disclosure that this
is AI, not drone footage. Close it. Tap "Floor plan": scan a room in 3D on a
LiDAR iPhone, or upload a plan.

Say: Add an aerial intro — an AI opening shot that lifts off an exterior photo,
always disclosed as AI-generated. And a floor plan: scan a room in 3D on
supported iPhones, or upload one you already have.

Caption: Aerial intro (disclosed as AI) · Floor plan: scan in 3D or upload

## 11 · 1:56–2:08 · Publish, share, leads

On screen: Home's Leads banner — "Every tour is one link with a lead form built
in. Leads appear here." Tap it: the Leads screen (empty on a fresh install).

Say: When the tour is ready, publish it. You get a share link — and for real
estate, a separate unbranded link that's safe for the MLS. Every enquiry from
that page lands here, in Leads.

Caption: Publish → share link (+ MLS-safe link) · leads land in the app

## 12 · 2:08–2:18 · Plans and the free trial

On screen: Settings → Plan & usage → "Upgrade plan". The plans and their prices,
Monthly / Yearly. Close.

Say: Rendprop is free to download and try. Plans start at $49 a month, and every
plan begins with a 7-day free trial.

Caption: Free to try · plans from $49 a month · 7-day free trial

## 13 · 2:18–2:25 · The honest line

On screen: Back on Home, resting on the hero.

Say: AI-altered media is always labelled — in the app and on every tour you
publish. Rendprop, on the App Store.

Caption: AI-altered media is always labelled

## Notes for whoever records the voice

- Pace: about 2.5–3 words a second. Every line above is under 3.5 words/second
  for its slot; if a take runs long the build holds the frame, so don't rush.
- Pronounce it "Rend-prop". "MLS" is spelled out. "$49" is "forty-nine dollars".
- One file per segment: `narration/01.mp3` … `narration/13.mp3` (`.wav`/`.m4a`
  also fine). Segments 00 and 99 have no narration.
- Say only what is on screen. If a step is cut from the recording (its
  `TOUR_MARK` is missing), the build drops that segment's narration too.
