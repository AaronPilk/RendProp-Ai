RENDPROP — INSTAGRAM POSTS
20 carousels, ready to go out.


HOW TO POST ONE

Open a folder. Inside:

  1.jpg through 5.jpg   the slides, in swipe order
  caption.txt           the caption, copy the whole thing
  preview.jpg           all five side by side, so you can see the post
                        before you upload it

In Instagram, pick Post, select all five images, and check the order
before you hit next. Instagram usually keeps the order you tapped them
in, not the filename order, so tap 1 through 5 one at a time.

Paste the caption. Swap #yourcity for your actual market tag. Post it.


THE ORDER

Post them 01 through 20. The order isn't the order they were made in,
it's the order that works.

Four of the first eight are before-and-afters, because those are the
ones that stop a scroll and they're what you want on the page while
somebody is deciding whether to follow.

The rest of the order is about the grid. Your profile shows three
across, and Instagram crops each post square from the middle, so the
middle of every cover photo is a tile in that grid. Posts that lead
with the same house are spaced at least five apart, which keeps them
out of the same row and off each other's shoulder. That's why the
running order looks shuffled. Don't resort it alphabetically.

Two or three a week. Don't burn all twenty in a fortnight.


ONE RULE ABOUT THE PHOTOS

The houses are AI-generated for this brand. They're demonstrations of
what the app does to a photo, not properties you sold.

So never caption one as your listing. "This is what Rendprop does to a
photo" is honest. "My listing at 24 Willow Bend" is not, and it would
undercut the whole labelling position the app is built on, which is
post 10.


WHAT'S NOT IN HERE

Nothing in these posts advertises a feature that isn't live. The
agent-on-camera reel is server-side only right now and the 3D walk-around
tour is specced but not built, so both stay off the feed until they ship.

Source files that rebuild all of this live in the repo at
docs/marketing/instagram-carousels.
