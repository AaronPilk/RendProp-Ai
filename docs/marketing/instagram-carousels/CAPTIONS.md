# Captions — 20 posts

Written to be pasted straight in. First line is the hook (that's the only line
that shows before "more"), so don't move it. Hashtags sit at the bottom; swap
in your own market tags where it says `#yourcity`.

House rule: never caption these photos as a listing you sold or marketed. They
are generated demo homes. "This is what the app does to a photo" is honest.
"My listing at 24 Willow Bend" is not, and it torches the AI-labelling stance
the app is built on.

Your phone already shoots 4K. It just doesn't know how to move.

## 02 — The share link

The tour is a link. That's the whole trick.

Text it. Drop it in the MLS remarks. Put it in the email blast. It opens in Safari, Chrome, whatever your buyer already has open, on a five-year-old Android or a brand new iPhone.

Nobody downloads anything. Nobody signs up. They just look at the house.

Half the tours out there die at the "install our app to continue" screen. This one doesn't have one.

## 03 — Room tags

Buyers don't watch tours. They skim them.

So tag the rooms. Kitchen, primary, garage, backyard. Now the tour has a menu — tap the room you care about and land in it.

The couple who only care about the kitchen get the kitchen in one tap instead of scrubbing a two-minute video hunting for it.

Fewer people bounce. That's the entire point.
