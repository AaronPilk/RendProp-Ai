## The five before/after posts are the ones to lead with

04, 05, 06, 07 and 08 each show the SAME house or room, same camera, same
framing, with only the advertised change. That is what makes the claim legible
in a feed — a different photo on each side proves nothing. These will outperform
the others; consider posting one of them first rather than starting at 01.

## Photography

The listing photos are AI-generated for this brand: one modern home shot once,
then edited so every before/after pair is genuinely the same property. They are
demonstration imagery of what the app produces, not real listings.

**Do not caption them as a property you sold or marketed.** Given that the app's
own compliance position is that AI-altered media gets labelled, presenting
generated homes as real listings on the brand's own feed would undercut it. Say
"what Rendprop does to a photo", never "my listing at 24 Willow Bend".

## Copy

Every claim is taken from the shipped App Store description. Nothing here

only) and the 3D spatial tour (specced, not built) are deliberately absent.
