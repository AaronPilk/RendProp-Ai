# October 7 paid-launch candidate

Work is isolated on `fix/paid-launch-readiness-20261007`, after the source used
for internal TestFlight 1.0.4 (48). Build 48 does not contain these later changes.
Final CI, hosted rollout, signed build and physical-phone acceptance must be
recorded separately. This document does not authorize money or activate flags.

The candidate closes permanent StoreKit retry loops, resumes duplicate verified
Apple notifications without duplicate funding, and permits retail guest usage
only through the exact verified production purchase/funding binding. Recovery
keeps unfinished purchase custody and exposes Restore/Retry/Support.

Photo requests have a bounded paid fallback chain and prospective image/prompt
limits. Optional service-provisioned photo partitions protect included photo
admissions from helper usage. They are empty until an actual funding interval
and package are deliberately provisioned. Counts represent accepted requests,
including accepted failures; they are not guaranteed successful image outputs.
Account and workspace deletion cannot replenish them. Existing QA remains separate.

The approved trial experience remains seven days or its usage limit, whichever
comes first: one own captured/imported walkthrough up to 90 seconds, five photo
admissions, one distinct published listing and one GiB lifetime ingress. Using
up that allowance never advances Apple's paid billing date. Trial sponsorship
and account startup funding still need a concrete owner-approved allocation;
a supplier balance alone is not that approval.

Private media uses bounded actor/workspace/object capabilities with fresh
membership and provenance checks. Native and Studio adapters are part of the
candidate. Media delivery/read operations and storage admission are journaled
before consuming their funded allowance. Historical unbudgeted testers remain
separate. Public/private gateway flags remain off until an inventory, legacy
URL replacement and actual consumer acceptance establish a safe cutover.
No raw media domain shutdown, cleanup activation or customer-media deletion is
included in this rollout.

The owner selected preparation of a monthly/trial launch under a $290 review
ceiling. This does not authorize a charge or certify prepaid account coverage,
and annual-capable launch funding was not selected. The proposed 5/20/60 paid
photo counts were not approved: the owner requested an explanation of the large
reduction. Current plan quantities remain unchanged while predictable fixed-price
photo routes and useful allowances are reviewed. A worst-case reservation must
not be presented as an average supplier invoice or proof of market fit.

Modern Supabase server keys authenticate by exact configured API key, rather
than trusting JWT role text. Only notify, presenter-drain, me, uploads and tours
need the explicit application-authentication deployment transition; all other
declared policies remain unchanged. Three existing database drains preserve
their behavior while modernizing headers. Python credentialed requests refuse
redirects; source-bound localhost tests detect removed guards. Current Vault
credential mismatch, replacement readback and actual negative revocation probes
remain separate live gates. Provider key replacement must finish through the
owner's prepared secret handoff.

Known shared-workspace account-deletion refusals now explain the required
ownership/reassignment/support step. Unknown server diagnostics remain hidden,
and failures preserve the account and local files. Contact support opens a user
controlled email composer; it does not send mail automatically.

Spatial reconstruction, LiDAR/camera quality, two-device account acceptance,
actual StoreKit transactions, customer inbox delivery and provider output
quality require their own real-device/account evidence. They cannot be certified
by synthetic fixtures or simulator checks.

retained. The follow-up repairs keep the production admission and authentication
guards: they update precise fixture RPCs, exercise adoption replay at its real
migration boundary, and permit the documented 429 response in the tour host's
TypeScript response helpers. Media budget provisioning now also refuses a storage
cap below physically retained media. Actual local PostgreSQL tests, overlapping

SDK compile passes after preserving main-actor isolation on the pre-dispatch
callback. These local results require fresh clean-source hosted CI before rollout.
