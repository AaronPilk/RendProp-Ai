# Card-backed first-100 trial authority — source-only handoff

The owner approved a **$1,000 operating spend limit for the first 100 seven-day trials**, funded through valid payment cards and Kie/FAL auto-refill. This does **not** mean depositing $1,000 with a provider or claiming $1,000 already paid. The earlier paid-cash path remains available to an operator who actually has an immutable paid-cash receipt. This new path records `cash_paid_cents = 0` and a separate card-backed maximum liability.

Migration `20261028213014_trial_card_backed_authority.sql` is additive and dormant. Its SQL was applied under this production history version without seeding authority. It creates one nonrenewable card authority for the operator, at most $500 of 100 immutable $5 introductory holds and at most $500 of shared media/QA/legacy commitments. The existing per-actor, confirmed-identity and workspace uniqueness, seven-day product schedules, trial photo limit, signed-Apple conversion, provider cost reservation and fail-closed dispatch are unchanged. A revoked or stale provider verification refuses **new** holds; a previously admitted and signed seven-day purchase can still convert and receive its reserved media budget. The service role cannot directly write the authority or verification tables. No provider/card detail or private evidence file is stored in Git.

The SQL limit bounds **this launch cohort's admitted commitments**; it cannot itself cap unrelated supplier calls, existing paid customers, tax, card interest, or a provider dashboard that charges outside Rendprop. Keep provider/account spend alerts and the existing cost ledger active. A screenshot or owner statement is not a machine verification: the operator must inspect Kie and FAL separately, retain private dated evidence, and supply only SHA-256 digests to the database.
