### Decisions for Aaron (none lower the margin; all one-line config changes)

1. **30 % commission makes the advertised Starter/Pro allowances unreachable in one window.** At catalog prices a maxed Starter month costs ≈ 913¢ (100 × 6.7¢ photos + 6 × 24.3¢ reels + 2 × 48.6¢ aerials) against an 807¢ envelope; Pro ≈ 1,826¢ against 1,682¢; Team ≈ 3,650¢ fits 4,307¢. At 15 % (991¢ / 2,053¢) both fit. So until the Small Business rate is confirmed, a Starter/Pro customer who uses *everything* hits the money gate at roughly 88 % / 92 % of the meters with the "resets with your subscription period" copy. Options: (a) accept it for launch (margin first — my default); (b) flip to 15 % the day the first payout shows it: `update public.app_config set value = value || '{"apple_commission_bps":1500}'::jsonb, updated_at = now() where key = 'serving_envelope';` (no deploy); (c) trim the advertised reel/aerial counts on the site/App Store; (d) lower `net_margin_bps` (I would not).
2. **Trial pool ends 2026-11-08.** Trials are refused `[pool=closed]` after that until a new allocation is recorded: `update public.app_config set value = value || '{"cap_cents":29000,"starts_at":"2026-11-08T00:00:00Z","ends_at":"2026-12-08T00:00:00Z"}'::jsonb, updated_at = now() where key = 'trial_sponsor_pool';` — the `trial_pool_ending` alert reminds you from Nov 1. The trial plan's meters (60 photos / 4 reels / 2 aerials) are worth ≈ 596¢ at catalog, so a trial that uses everything is stopped by the 500¢ window first, as intended.
3. **Lapsed subscribers' free allowance counts their earlier spend.** The free tier is 300¢ *lifetime*; Richard Tocado's workspace has 396¢ of lifetime cost from his intro week, so every AI request there is refused with the free copy until he subscribes (or gets a testing grant if he is a launch tester). I kept it: the free tier is a sample, not a landing pad after churn. If you want lapsed subscribers to get a fresh sample, say so and I will count only spend after the last paid window.
4. **Pre-policy hosting.** His enrollment is gone; his 6 listings stay hosted with no deadline under the pre-Oct-6 arrangement. Any new subscription from him would be enrolled on the policy (90 days after expiry).
