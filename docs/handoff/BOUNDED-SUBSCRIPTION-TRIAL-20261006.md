# Dormant bounded subscription trial — 2026-10-06

This change implements product limits without activating or funding an offer. The migration inserts `enabled=false`; it seeds no Apple serving schedules, sponsor pools, owner dollars, or customer credits. `/me` returns `trial_offer:null` until the exact owner has acquired a funded pre-purchase hold; no loose availability is advertised. The proposed owner spending limits of $5 per trial and $25 shared remain unapproved.

The immutable trial is registered in the same transaction as accepted, funded Production Apple `FREE_TRIAL` funding. It binds the confirmed account identity, original Apple chain, workspace, and funding receipt. It ends at the earlier verified receipt expiry or seven days. Deletion, another workspace/device, a month boundary, another original chain, or receipt restore cannot replenish it. Minimal account-related actor/workspace UUIDs, Apple chain/funding identifiers, a one-way confirmed-identity digest and admission tombstones survive deletion; raw email, input media, authentication material and signed capabilities are not stored in those tables.

The dormant snapshot contains one walkthrough from existing footage, five photo admissions, one distinct published listing, and 1GiB of lifetime upload transport holds. Photo fallbacks within one logical operation consume one admission; every provider attempt independently reserves money. An admission is not a guaranteed successful image. Definitive provider rejection may release its financial hold, while successful or ambiguous liabilities remain; the lifetime admission is retained. Unpriced routes still fail closed. Paid video/reel/aerial/upscale/spatial stages are excluded from new bounded trials; `ai-enhance` remains authenticated and closed.

SQL triggers enforce paid-attempt admission, lifetime ingress, render-job admission and actual publication. Another member cannot borrow the named trial owner's ingress, and an actor cannot evade trial limits by switching to an unfunded free workspace. New hosted publication requires verified current paid funding, a registered funded trial, or an explicit private/manual/contract authority. Existing recorded job/publication retries return before insertion; another job for the same admitted asset does not consume another walkthrough. New uploads stop at the lifetime ceiling, but full ingress does not hide usable photo/publication buckets. Exhausted means all three action buckets are consumed; expiry and current actor/funding authority are also checked. Legacy cancelled zero-hold v1 cleanup remains available without refunding or adding trial bytes.

`/me` exposes `trial_usage` scoped to its enclosing workspace, or null after a verified paid renewal/explicit override. Each bucket has `used`, `cap`, and `remaining`; exact starts/ends and upload holds are separate. `serving_activation` contains `org_id`, `available`, `funded`, and `authority`. Current verified retail has precedence over any historical overlapping sponsor row, including for another current member. An unfunded active Apple plan retains its signed/raw plan but exposes zero usable monthly caps and `entitlement.degraded:true`. An active Production free-trial sync without service activation returns a clear 503 after preserving Apple's chronology. Expired/free clients should prioritize expired trial history over an activation-pending label. Explicit non-Apple/manual/private/contract presentation does not invent collected retail proceeds or a 75% margin proof.

Genuinely funded pre-config trials retain their previous publication and quota semantics for the exact named, nonanonymous current editor. Publication requires a current unrevoked finite sponsor allocation and a current positive-budget slice; an unfunded nominal trial, bare historical row, new unregistered allocation, other member, revoked or expired sponsor cannot claim that compatibility path. The existing legacy three-render quota remains independently enforced.

Owned admission metadata is included in account export without live listing/asset references, so deleted listings do not make an export permanently fail. Saved work, existing media/read/export and links remain governed by the existing authority and hosting-retention policy; this change does not delete them.

## Activation gates

- The additive [purchase reservation](TRIAL-PURCHASE-RESERVATIONS-20261006.md) implements atomic pre-StoreKit cash commitment and exact buyer conversion. Its configuration/pools remain disabled and unseeded; real Apple acceptance and reviewed deployment remain required.
- Owner funding, sponsor schedules and all seven non-AI reserve categories must be approved and verified. Five admissions do not certify the cash needed for five successful edits, retries, ambiguous invoices, storage, delivery, compute, email, support, retention and uncertainty.
- The separately reviewed213000 duration overlay verifies the exact retained MP4 object before trial publication. Its probe/attestation and the funded purchase path must be deployed together before any offer is enabled.
- Existing marketed paid feature bundles remain a separate funding/pricing gate. This trial source does not change prices, Apple SKUs, or claim those bundles are financially certified.
