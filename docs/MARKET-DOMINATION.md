# RENDPROP — MARKET DOMINATION STRATEGY
*Deep research completed 2026-08-26. Mission: make external marketing agencies and per-listing media vendors unnecessary for real-estate agents and property developers. All prices verified against live pages or 2026 published reviews.*

---

## 1. The market

- **~1.44M Realtors** (NAR, June 2026). Median agent closes ~5–6 listings/yr; the 3–5 listings/MONTH producer is the top ~5–10% → **~75–150K core ICP agents**, plus ~100K media pros.
- Agent marketing spend: avg **$12–14.2K/yr**; 52% of $300K+ GCI agents budget $20K+/yr. Total US agent marketing market: **$4.2B–$16.2B/yr**.
- **The adoption gap is the wedge:** listings with video get 403% more inquiries and 85% of buyers rank photos #1 — yet only **9% of agents make listing videos**. Most of the market isn't switching vendors; they're getting video for the first time at a price that finally works.
- Developers: multifamily lease-up marketing norm **≥0.5% of project cost** (~$250K on a $50M / 200-unit project); renderings $500–$2,500/still, a 60s film $8–12K, pre-leasing programs $50–150K.

## 2. What agents pay today (validated benchmarks)

Photos $150–300/shoot · video walkthrough $250–600 (cinematic $500–1,500) · drone $100–350 · Matterport service scan $350–1,000 (or $6K camera + subs) · virtual twilight $5–25 · virtual staging $15–50/photo · single-property site $9–150/mo · RE social-media management retainers **$2,500–5,000/mo** · Luxury Presence up to $1,500/mo · Zillow Showcase ~$400/mo and $300–1,150 per extra listing.

**The "$2–3K/month agency" claim is VALIDATED.** Full-service RE marketing retainers run $2,500–5,000/mo before ad spend; a 4-listing/mo producer buying media à la carte spends $3,460–7,100/mo, $4.5–10K all-in with social. Use "$2,500/mo agency vs $99/mo Rendprop" in copy — it survives scrutiny.

## 3. Competitor map (and how we beat each)

- **AutoReel** ($19–49/mo) — the one to watch: best flythroughs from photos, Zillow import, API, PR budget. **No video capture, no landing page, no leads, no CRM, no native iOS.**
- **Reel-E** ($44–599/mo) — 4 reel variants per upload, loud content marketing. Photos only, no pages/leads.
- **Rendy** (invite-only) — sells AI video to *photographers* as a $75–150/shoot upsell. Not agent-facing; it arms a channel, not a competitor product.
- **Arvaum** ($29–149/mo) — desktop AI photo editing only; its "video" tab is literally a Rendy referral link. **Mirino** — negligible footprint.
- **BoxBrownie/Styldod/VirtualStagingAI** — per-photo human/AI editing, 24–48h turnaround. We're seconds and included.
- **Matterport/iGuide** — hardware + subs; and **Zillow killed third-party tours in listings (late 2025)** → "own your tour link" is now a live wound we market to.
- **Luxury Presence / Lofty / kvCORE** ($300–1,500/mo) — agent websites/CRM. They market the *agent*; none create listing media.
- **Brokerage in-house platforms** (Compass Home Platform, July 2026) — the real enemy at big brokerages; counter with franchise wholesale deals.

**Nobody — nobody — has the full chain: capture → drone-style tour → the link IS a full listing landing page → lead capture → CRM → analytics → photos → reels, in one native iOS app.** Every competitor is a single-feature widget. That chain is the moat; the reels feature exists to make Rendprop the DAILY app, not a per-listing tool.

- Team **$249** (5 seats, shared brand kit, 25 listings/mo)

**Unlimited** = anything near-zero marginal cost (hosted pages, leads, analytics, sharing, floor-plan scans, template reels). **Credit-metered** = genuinely expensive ops (generative video minutes, AI flyovers, 4K upscales, voiceover), pools sized so 90% never hit them. 1. **Listing-presentation wedge** — Zillow's own data: rich-media users win 30% more listings. Sell Rendprop as a listing-WINNING tool; ship "listing presentation mode."
2. **Compliance-as-a-feature** — CA AB 723 (1/1/26) + ARMLS (5/28/26) require AI-image disclosure + original access. Auto-embed disclosures + keep before/after pairs → become the tool broker counsel approves. (Product already labels AI content; formalize per-MLS presets.)
3. **Brokerage wholesale** — $29–39/agent enterprise tier, RE/MAX-style deals, before Compass-style in-house locks them out.
4. **Developer flank** — nobody sells AI listing media to lease-up teams; highest ACV, least competition; our multi-industry architecture already fits.
5. **Every shared link is an ad** — "Marketed with Rendprop" footer on lower tiers; price removal, not sharing.
6. **Photographer channel** (Rendy's proof): white-label "Pro Media" reseller mode instead of fighting photographers.
7. **Comparison-content SEO war** — out-publish AutoReel/Reel-E's funnel: per-city cost calculators, vs-pages, the $2,500-vs-$99 math page (rendprop.com/compare already live; expand).
8. **GoHighLevel marketplace** — leads already sync to GHL; list there and turn agencies into distribution.
9. **Zillow-independence campaign** — "Zillow deleted everyone's Matterport tours. Own your tour. Own your leads."
10. **Native-capture moat** — only capture-first native iOS product in category; own App Store search; ship weekly.

## 7. Risks + mitigations

- **AI-disclosure regime** (AB 723, MLS rules, ~$250/violation): turn into moat (#2). Never alter structural features; keep originals.
- **Photo copyright** (photographer-owned MLS photos + litigious incumbents): require rights confirmation on import; agent-shot capture is copyright-clean by design.
- **Zillow platform power**: never build features that depend on portal embedding; win around the portal.
- **Quality bar**: "no hallucinations" is table stakes; interior-motion quality benchmarks before marketing claims (anti-hallucination prompt scaffolding already shipped in ai-video).
- **AI cost exposure**: monthly per-org caps now enforced server-side; credit design per §5.
- **Apple fees** (proposed 5–15% on external-link purchases, Aug 2026, pending court): sell Team/Agency/Developer via web Stripe, keep Solo/Pro IAP; re-check ruling at launch.
