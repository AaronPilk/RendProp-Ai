# rendprop.com keyword list and first content plan, 2026-11-12

Step 12 of the SEO SOP. This is the first keyword list for rendprop.com, the
page that should rank for each search, and the first five new pages to build.

## How this list was made

- **What the site already covers.** The marketing pages (`/`, `/features`,
  `/pricing`, `/compare`, `/support`), the four `/compare/*` pages and the 432
  blog posts in `services/edge/tour-host/content/blog/` (27 in each of 16
  topics). The blog is almost all informational: "what is", "how much does",
  "should I", and how-tos for the app. It already answers most question
  searches.
- **What agents search and what ranks.** Web searches on 2026-11-12 for listing
  video from photos, single property websites, real estate photo editing and
  virtual staging apps, real estate reels, MLS unbranded tour links, Tampa and
  St. Petersburg real estate photography, and the AutoReel, Mirino, Arvaum and
  Rendy alternatives. No volume tool was used, so there are no search-volume
  numbers here. Intent and the type of page that ranks were read from the
  results themselves.
- **What the results showed.** For the commercial searches ("real estate video
  from photos", "single property website", "real estate photo editing app",
  "virtual staging app") page one is mostly product landing pages and vendor
  listicles: AutoReel has a dedicated "AI video from listing photos" page,
  Luxury Presence and AgentFire have single-property-website product pages,
  imagen and virtualstaging.art run "best app" lists. rendprop.com had no
  product page for any of these, only blog answers. That gap is where the
  first pages go. Local searches show national networks' city pages (HomeJab,
  Virtuance) next to local studios.

## The first five new pages, in priority order

1. **`/listing-video-from-photos`**, "Listing Video From Photos". The core
   product job with the clearest buying intent, and the ranking pages are
   product pages (AutoReel's is one). Rendprop only had blog answers for it.
   *Built 2026-11-12.*
2. **`/single-property-website`**, "Single Property Websites for Real Estate
   Agents". It comes with every plan and no AI reel maker in the comparison
   table offers it, yet the site had no product page for a search led by
   vendor product pages. *Built 2026-11-12.*
3. **`/real-estate-photo-editing-app`**, "Real Estate Photo Editing & Virtual
   Staging App". Two large commercial searches with one honest answer: iPhone
   edits, a label on every edit and the original kept. The blog covers the
   rules, not the product. *Built 2026-11-12.*
4. **`/tampa-bay-real-estate-photography`**, the on-site service page. Local
   searches convert and the competition is beatable, but **it is not built**
   because the repo has no local proof: no real jobs, no photos from real
   shoots, no packages or prices, no booking method and no list of towns
   served. A city page without those is a doorway page. It needs from the
   owner: 6 to 10 real shoots with addresses the sellers agreed to show, the
   photos and videos from them, what is offered and at what price, how to book,
   and which towns are actually covered.
5. **`/mls-unbranded-tour-link`**, an answer guide to which link goes in the
   MLS virtual tour field. Rendprop's unbranded link is the one row in the
   comparison table no other tool has, the searches are specific, and all the
   facts are already on the site (features, support, llms.txt). It can be
   built next without owner input.

Next after these: a dedicated vertical reels page ("real estate reels app")
once New 1 shows whether the reels searches land on it or need their own page.

## Rules the new pages follow

- Facts come from the site and repo only: prices, plans and the trial from
  `public/pricing.html`; features from `public/index.html`,
  `public/features.html`, `public/compare.html`, `public/support.html` and
  `public/llms.txt`. Market rates are the August 2026 typical U.S. rates
  already quoted on `/pricing` and `/`.
- No invented prices, counts, reviews, awards, turnaround times or service
  areas.
- Each page has one H1 with the answer first, a visible Updated date, a
  visible FAQ that matches its FAQPage JSON-LD, links to related pages and to
  `/pricing`, and a `SITE_PAGES` entry in `src/sitemap.ts`.
