What is a listing launch

A listing launch is the coordinated first release of a home's marketing: photos, video, page, MLS entry, links, posts and email, timed to land together.

October 28, 2026 · 4 min read · by Rendprop

Short answer. A listing launch is the coordinated first release of a home's marketing, timed so that the photos, the video, the property page, the MLS entry, the two links, the social posts and the email to your database all go out together, usually within a day or two. The point is to concentrate attention in the first week, when a listing gets the most views it will ever get.

A listing launch is the coordinated first release of a home's marketing, timed so the photos, the video, the property page, the MLS entry, the links, the social posts and the email to your database all go out together, usually inside a day or two. The purpose is to concentrate attention in the first week, when a listing gets more views than it will in any later week.

A launch is a sequence with an order, not a pile of tasks. The page comes before the posts because the posts need the link.

How a launch runs

It starts when the photos are delivered. From those photos you build the listing video and, if you post to social, a vertical reel. You publish the property page with the gallery, the video and the details, which gives you two links: branded for your own channels and unbranded for the MLS. You enter the listing in the MLS with the unbranded link in the virtual tour field. Then, and only then, the announcement goes out: the just listed reel to your social accounts with the branded link in the bio, and the email to your database with the same link. The sign goes in with a QR code to the page. The first open house is set and announced within the same week.

Each step feeds the next, which is why a launch done out of order produces a reel pointing at a page that is not live yet.

When the timing matters most

  • A fast market, where the first weekend decides the outcome and every channel should be live before Saturday.
  • A slow market, where the launch is the one moment you can count on attention and a soft start wastes it.
  • A relaunch after an expiry or a failed contract, where the point is to make the home look new again rather than tired.

What a launch is not

A launch is not the whole marketing plan. It is the first week. Price changes, open houses, under contract and sold posts come later and reuse the same media. It is also not just the MLS entry; a listing that appears in the MLS with no page, no video and no announcement has been entered, not launched.

The nearest confusable thing is a coming soon period. That is pre-marketing before the listing is active, and your MLS's current rules decide what you may do during it. A launch is what happens the day the listing goes live.

A worked example: Tuesday listing, Saturday open house

Saturday: the photographer shoots a 3-bed in a cul-de-sac and delivers twenty-six landscape photos and eight portrait ones by evening. Sunday: the agent reviews two AI edits, a blue sky on the front exterior and a tidied kitchen, compares them with the originals, keeps both, and builds the landscape video and a 22-second vertical reel. Monday: she publishes the property page, checks it on her phone, copies both links, and enters the listing in the MLS to go live Tuesday at 8 am with the unbranded link in the tour field.

Tuesday 8 am: the listing is live. 8:30: the just listed reel posts with the branded link in her bio. 9:00: the database email goes out with the same link. Noon: the sign goes in with the QR code. Wednesday: an open house announcement cut goes out for Saturday one to three. By Thursday the page has produced four enquiries, three of them about Saturday. The launch took four days of small steps in the right order.

Where launches go wrong

Posting before the page is live. Pasting the branded link into the MLS and the unbranded link into the email. Announcing on social and forgetting the database, which is where your past clients are. Launching on a Friday evening when nobody is looking. Or spreading the steps across two weeks so there is no first week at all. Order the steps, pick a launch morning, and have the page and links ready the night before.

Next step: write your launch order on one line, photos, video, page, links, MLS, reel, email, sign, and pin it where you will see it the night before your next listing goes live.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

What is included in a listing launch?

The photos, the listing video and reel, the property page, the MLS entry with the unbranded link, the just listed post with the branded link, the email to your database, the sign with a QR code and the first open house announcement. All within the first day or two.

What order should the launch steps go in?

Photos, then video, then the property page, then the two links, then the MLS entry, then the reel and the email, then the sign. The page comes before any post because every post needs the link.

What day of the week is best to launch a listing?

Early in the week, so the first open house can be the coming weekend and the announcement has several days to circulate. Avoid Friday evenings and holiday weekends unless the market is unusually fast.

Is a coming soon period part of the launch?

It is pre-marketing before the launch. Your MLS's current rules decide what you may do during it, so check them. The launch itself is the day the listing goes live and every channel points to the page.
