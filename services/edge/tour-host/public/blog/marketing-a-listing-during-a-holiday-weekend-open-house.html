Marketing a listing during a holiday weekend open house

A holiday weekend splits buyers: locals leave, visitors arrive. Hold the open house Saturday morning, and send the page and video to everyone who is away.

Post before they travel, follow up the Tuesday after, and do not read a quiet Sunday as a quiet listing.

A holiday weekend splits your buyers into two groups: the locals who leave town and the visitors who arrive, often staying with people who live near the listing. Run the open house on Saturday morning, before the barbecues and the drive home, for the people who are around. Put the property page and the video in front of everyone who is away, before they leave and again on Tuesday. A quiet Sunday afternoon on a long weekend is not a verdict on the listing.

Who is actually in town

Think about the three days as different audiences. The locals who are still at home on Saturday morning are the ones with nothing booked, and some of them are buyers who finally have a free morning. The visitors staying with family or friends nearby are a group most agents forget; a buyer from two hours away who is staying three streets over will walk past an open house sign. And the locals who left on Friday are still buyers; they are just browsing from a deck chair with a phone, which is where the page and the video earn their keep.

Plan for all three. The open house serves the first two. The link serves the third.

Saturday morning, not Sunday afternoon

On a normal weekend the Sunday afternoon open house is the habit. On a holiday weekend Sunday is the day of the cookout and Monday is the drive home, so Saturday from ten to noon is the slot with the most people free and the fewest competing plans. It also leaves the sellers their weekend.

  • Signs out Friday evening. Directional signs up before the visitors arrive on Friday night so they are seen on the Saturday morning coffee run.
  • A sign-in that captures the visitors. Out-of-town visitors are the ones most likely to give a real email because they cannot come back next week. Point the sign-in at the property page so they leave with the link. How that works end to end is in open house sign-in that follows up itself.
  • A QR code to the page on the flyer and the sign. A visitor who is leaving on Monday takes the link home, not the flyer.
  • Keep it short. Two hours. A six-hour holiday open house exhausts the sellers for a trickle of afternoon traffic.

The page does the work for everyone who left

The posting schedule for a holiday weekend is front-loaded. The just-listed email and the first reel go out Thursday, when people are still at their desks and planning. A second post Saturday morning with the open house time catches the locals who stayed. Then nothing on Sunday and Monday, because nobody is reading, and a follow-up on Tuesday morning when everyone is back and catching up on what they missed.

The video matters more than usual on these weekends. A buyer on a sofa in another city with a long weekend and a phone will watch a two-minute listing video they would not have watched on a Tuesday. Make sure it is on the page, and make sure the page is what you are sending, not a zip of photos. The general approach to building the open house around existing photos is in open house marketing from the photos you already have.

The lake house on Memorial Day weekend

A three-bed on a small lake, forty minutes from the city, listed the Tuesday before Memorial Day. The agent's instinct was to skip the weekend. Instead: just-listed email and the reel out Thursday morning, directional signs placed Friday at five, open house Saturday ten to noon.

Eleven groups came through. Four were neighbours. Three were locals with nothing planned. Four were visitors staying with relatives on the lake for the weekend, two of whom had been thinking about a lake place for years and had never seen one with a sign out on a day they were there. All four visiting groups scanned the QR code and left with the page. Sunday and Monday were silent. On Tuesday morning the agent sent one message to everyone from the sign-in: the page link, the video, and an offer of a private showing the following weekend or a video call sooner. One of the visiting couples booked a video call for Wednesday and a showing for the Saturday after, and that is the showing that produced the offer. The open house that almost did not happen found the buyer who lived two hours away.

Holiday weekend mistakes

Skipping the weekend entirely. You lose the visitors, who are the one audience you cannot reach any other way.

Running it Sunday afternoon out of habit. You get the leftover hour between the cookout and the dishes.

Posting through the weekend. Three reels on Sunday reach nobody and look like the listing is trying too hard. Thursday, Saturday morning, Tuesday.

Reading the quiet as a signal. Sellers see no showings on Sunday and want a price cut on Monday. Wait for Tuesday and the follow-up before anyone reads anything into the weekend.

Letting the sign-in be a clipboard. The visitors are the reason to run the open house, and the clipboard loses them the moment they get in the car.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

Is it worth holding an open house on a holiday weekend?

Yes, on Saturday morning. Locals who stayed home have a free morning, and visitors staying with family or friends nearby will walk past a sign. Those visitors are buyers you cannot reach any other way, and many of them cannot come back next week, so the sign-in and the page link matter more than usual.

What is the best time for a holiday weekend open house?

Saturday from about ten until noon. Sunday is the cookout and Monday is the drive home, so Saturday morning has the most people free and the fewest competing plans. Keep it to two hours.

When should I post about the listing around a holiday weekend?

Front-load it. The just-listed email and the first reel go out Thursday while people are still planning, a short post with the open house time goes out Saturday morning, and the follow-up goes out Tuesday when everyone is back. Nothing on Sunday or Monday.

Should I worry if a holiday weekend is quiet?

Not until Tuesday. Many buyers are away and browsing from a phone, which is why the page and the video need to be in front of them. Send the follow-up Tuesday morning with the page link and an offer of a showing or a video call, and judge the weekend after that lands.
