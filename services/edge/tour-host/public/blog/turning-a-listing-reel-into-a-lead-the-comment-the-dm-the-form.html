Turning a listing reel into a lead: the comment, the DM, the form

A reel viewer becomes a lead in three steps: a public reply with one fact, a private message with one question and the link, and the form on the page.

October 28, 2026 · 4 min read · by Rendprop

Short answer. A viewer becomes a lead in three moves. The comment gets a public reply with one specific fact. The private message asks one question and sends the property page link. The enquiry form on the page captures their details and the date they want to visit, and that submission in your inbox is the moment they stop being a viewer. Do not try to close in the comments.

A listing reel turns into a lead in three steps, in order: a public reply to the comment with one specific fact, a private message that asks one question and sends the property page link, and the enquiry form on that page, where the viewer leaves their details and the date they want to see the home. The form submission in your inbox is the point where a viewer becomes a lead. Nothing is closed in the comments and nobody is asked for a phone number in the first message.

Why three steps and not one

Each step is a slightly bigger ask, and each one filters. A comment costs nothing. A private message costs a little attention. Filling in a form with a date costs a real intention. Trying to skip from the comment to the showing request in one reply scares off the people who were only half interested, and half interested is where most buyers start.

The steps also move the conversation to where it belongs. The comment thread is public and read by everyone. The private message is where specifics get exchanged. The form is where the details are recorded in one place you will actually look at next week.

A townhouse, one viewer, three messages

A three-bed townhouse with a rooftop deck at $489,000. A viewer comments on the launch reel: Does the deck get sun in the afternoon?

Step one: the public reply

Reply in the thread: West-facing, so sun from about two until sunset in summer. The seller put the planters on the north rail for that reason. One fact, one detail that proves you have stood on the deck, no pitch.

Why public: everyone else wondering about the deck now knows, and the viewer sees a real answer rather than a DM me.

Step two: the private message

If the viewer replies in the thread with anything warm, send a private message: Glad that helped. Are you looking at townhouses in this area generally, or was it the deck that caught you? The full gallery and video are here, and there is a form on the page if you want to pick a day to see it. Then the branded property page link.

One question, so they have something to answer. The link, so they have somewhere to go. No phone number request. No are you working with an agent. Those come later, in person or on a call they chose.

Step three: the form

The viewer opens the page, watches the video, scrolls the gallery and fills in the enquiry form with the date they want to visit. That submission lands in your inbox attached to this listing. Now you have a lead: a person, a home and a date. You reply within the hour with two time options and the parking situation, and the first call happens because they asked for it. The reply pattern from there is in the first reply and the first call.

The messages that break the chain

  • Asking for a number in the first DM. It is the fastest way to end a conversation that was going fine.
  • Sending a portal link. It opens on a login prompt or another agent's name. Send your own property page, with your contact card on it.
  • Three questions in one message. Are you pre-approved, what is your timeline, have you seen anything else. The viewer answers none of them.
  • Leaving the form submission until Monday. A form filled in on Thursday evening and answered on Monday is a lead that found another home over the weekend.
  • Treating every commenter as a buyer. Some are neighbors, some are agents, some are the seller's cousin. The three steps sort them without you having to guess. The ones who fill in a form are the ones who meant it.
  • Closing in the thread. When can you come see it, posted publicly under a comment, reads as pressure to the viewer and as desperation to everyone else.

Keeping the thread honest

The public reply is also where the next buyer checks you. If the deck does not get afternoon sun, say east-facing, morning sun, and let the viewer decide. The point of the first step is that it is true and specific; a fact that turns out to be wrong at the showing ends the lead and the trust at the same time. FAQ

Questions on this topic

Should I ask for a phone number in the first DM?

No. Ask one question and send the property page link. The form on the page is where the viewer gives their details and a date, and the call happens after that because they asked for it.

What makes a reel viewer a lead rather than a commenter?

The form. A comment or a DM is interest; their contact details and a date they want to visit is intent. When that submission arrives in your inbox attached to the listing, you have a lead.

How quickly should I reply to a form submission from a reel?

Within the hour if you can, and the same day without exception. A viewer who filled in a form on Thursday night and heard back on Monday has usually moved on.

What if the commenter is another agent or a neighbor?

The three steps sort that out. A neighbor rarely fills in a form with a visit date; an agent will usually say so when you ask your one question. Answer them politely either way and move on.
