Open Settings in the Rendprop app. Settings is where you compare or change plans, and it is where Rendprop shows current plan features and included usage. Current plans and prices are on the Rendprop pricing page, and the app shows your available usage before you create.

When you choose a plan, Apple shows the price, billing period and any introductory offer terms before confirmation, and Apple's subscription management, reached from Settings, is where you change or cancel later.

What Settings holds

  • Compare or change plans. See what each current plan includes and switch if you want to.
  • Current features and included usage. What your plan covers and how much of it is included, as it stands now.
  • Restore purchases. If the app is not showing a plan you already bought, perhaps on a new phone, restore it here.
  • Apple's subscription management. The route to Apple's own screen for changing the billing period, cancelling or reviewing what you are paying.
  • Account deletion request. Separate from everything above; the deletion post covers it.

Checking your plan, step by step

  1. Open Rendprop and go to Settings.
  2. Open the plan comparison. Read the current features and the included usage for the plan you are on and the ones you are not.
  3. If you want to change, choose the plan. Apple then shows the price, the billing period and any introductory offer terms. Read that screen; it is the one that counts.
  4. Confirm with Apple, or back out. Nothing changes until you confirm.
  5. If a plan you have paid for is missing, use restore purchases before you buy anything again.

What to check before you confirm with Apple

The billing period, because different billing periods are different commitments. Any introductory offer terms, including what the price becomes after the introductory period. The Apple ID being charged, if you have a personal one and a business one on the same phone. And whether the included usage matches how you actually work: a photographer with four shoots on Saturday and an agent listing six homes a year are not on the same plan for the same reason.

A worked example: the agent sizing up before a busy spring

An agent with four listings coming in March opens Settings on a Sunday evening to see where she stands. She compares plans and reads the included usage for each against her plan: four listings, each with a landscape video and a page, two with a vertical social video. She decides whether her current plan covers that or whether a change makes sense, picks accordingly, and Apple shows the price, billing period and introductory terms before she confirms. She reads the screen, notes the renewal date in her calendar, and confirms. Ten minutes, done inside the app, no email to anyone.

In April she gets a new phone. Rendprop does not show her plan at first; she opens Settings, taps restore purchases, and it is back. She did not buy it twice.

Where plan management goes wrong

  • Reading a screenshot someone posted instead of Settings. Plans change; the app is current.
  • Confirming with Apple without reading the billing period. That screen is the contract. Read it.
  • Buying again on a new phone. Restore purchases first.
  • Trying to cancel by deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed through Apple's subscription management, reached from Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.
  • Using the wrong Apple ID. The plan is tied to the Apple ID that bought it. A team should buy on the business Apple ID it uses for the account.

What a plan does not change

How the listing works. The photo studio, the video step, the website step, the two links and the inbox are the same workflow whatever you are on; the plan governs features and included usage, which Settings shows. Your ownership of your photos and videos does not depend on a plan either. The features page describes the workflow, and the support page is the place for a question about a specific charge.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

Where do I see what my Rendprop plan includes?

In Settings in the app, where you can compare or change plans and see current plan features and included usage. Current plans and prices are on the Rendprop pricing page, and the app shows your available usage before you create.

Where is the price shown?

Through Apple's subscription management, which you can reach from Settings in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
