What is a just listed video

A just listed video is a short announcement reel telling your audience a home is new to market: price, area, three strong scenes and the page link.

October 28, 2026 · 4 min read · by Rendprop

Short answer. A just listed video is a short announcement video, usually vertical and under thirty seconds, that tells your followers and database a home has come to market. It leads with the fact that it is new, shows the three or four best scenes, states the price and the area, and sends people to the property page. A just listed video is a short announcement video, usually vertical and under thirty seconds, that tells your followers and your database a home has come to market. It leads with the fact that the listing is new, shows the three or four best scenes, states the price and the general area, and sends people to the property page. It is the opening move of a listing launch, not the whole campaign.

The job is narrow: make people who might care aware, today, and give them one place to go.

What goes into the twenty-five seconds

It uses the same photos as the listing video and the gallery, chosen for impact rather than completeness. The first frame carries the words Just listed or New to market with the strongest image behind it, often the exterior or the one room that sells the house. Then two or three scenes: kitchen, living space, the view or the yard. A short voiceover or on-screen text gives the price, the beds and baths and the neighbourhood. The last frame says where to go: link in bio, or the page link if the platform allows it.

Captions carry the facts for viewers on mute. Music is optional and quiet. The whole thing runs fifteen to thirty seconds because nobody needs a tour to learn a home exists.

When a just listed video goes out

  • The day the listing goes live, timed with the MLS entry so the page and the link are ready.
  • Again, in a different cut, once the first weekend has passed and the first open house is set.
  • For a coming soon period, if your MLS's current rules allow pre-marketing and you follow them.

It is the wrong format once the home has been on the market for a while; at that point the message is the price, the open house or what changed, not that it is new.

What a just listed video is not

A just listed video is not the listing video. The listing video walks a buyer through the home and lives on the property page; the announcement tells people the home exists and lives in the feed. It is not an open house reel, which is about a date and a time, and it is not a just sold post, which comes after closing.

The nearest confusable thing is a general new listing post with a single photo and a paragraph of text. The video version shows more in less time and gets a different kind of attention in a feed, but it carries the same message and points to the same page.

A Tuesday launch and a Thursday recut

A 3-bed in a cul-de-sac goes live on a Tuesday morning. The agent has twenty-six landscape photos and eight portrait ones from Saturday's shoot, and the property page was published Monday night. Tuesday at eight she builds a 22-second vertical video: Just listed over the front exterior, then the kitchen, then the deck, then the primary bedroom. The voiceover says the price, three beds, two baths, the neighbourhood name, and open house Sunday. The last frame says Link in bio.

It posts to Instagram and TikTok by nine with the branded page link in the bio. The same morning the just listed email to her database goes out with the page link and a still from the video. By Tuesday evening two enquiries have arrived through the page, both asking about Sunday. Thursday she builds a second cut with the open house time on the first frame from the same photos.

Where launch announcements go wrong

Posting before the page is live, so the link goes nowhere. Making it too long, so it becomes a tour nobody asked for. Leaving the price out, which invites a hundred comments asking for it and no enquiries. Pointing to a portal instead of your own page. Or opening on your own face instead of the home. Page first, price in, under thirty seconds, home in the first frame.

Next step: for your next launch, write the single first line of the just listed video before the photos arrive. If it is not the price or the one thing that makes the home different, rewrite it.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

How long should a just listed video be?

Fifteen to thirty seconds. It announces that the home exists and sends people to the page; it does not need to tour the whole house. Three or four scenes, the price, the area and a link are enough.

Should a just listed video include the price?

Yes. Leaving the price out produces comments asking for it rather than enquiries. State it in the voiceover and in the captions so viewers on mute see it too.

What is the difference between a just listed video and a listing video?

The listing video walks a buyer through the home and lives on the property page. The just listed video is a short announcement for the feed that sends people to that page. Both are built from the same photos.

When should the just listed video go out?

The day the listing goes live, after the property page is published so the link works. If your MLS's current rules allow coming soon marketing, a version can go out earlier, but follow those rules exactly.
