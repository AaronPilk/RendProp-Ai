What is a social reel for a listing

A social reel for a listing is a short vertical video made for phone feeds: a hook in the first seconds, a few scenes, captions and a link to tap.

October 28, 2026 · 3 min read · by Rendprop

Short answer. A social reel for a listing is a short vertical video, usually under a minute, made to be watched on a phone inside a feed. It opens with a hook, shows a handful of the home's best scenes, carries captions for viewers with the sound off, and ends by sending people to the property page. It is built for attention first and information second, which makes it a different object from the listing video on the property page.

A social reel for a listing is a short vertical video, usually under a minute, made to be watched on a phone inside a feed on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube Shorts. It opens with a hook, shows a handful of the home's best scenes, carries captions for viewers who have the sound off, and ends by pointing people to the property page. It is built for attention first and detail second.

That ordering is the whole definition. The listing video on the property page can take its time; the reel has about three seconds to earn the next ten.

How one is built

A reel is 9:16, taller than it is wide, so it fills the phone. The source material is portrait photos from the shoot, or a vertical cut of filmed footage. Six to ten scenes is typical. The first scene is the strongest image or the strongest line, not the front door. A voiceover or on-screen text carries one idea per scene: the price, the neighbourhood, the thing about the home that makes it different. Word-by-word captions keep the message readable with the sound off. Music sits underneath if it suits the home.

The end is a direct instruction: link in bio, tap for the page, open house Sunday at one.

When a reel is the right move

  • Launch day, as the first public look at the home.
  • Open house week, with the date and time on screen.
  • A price change, a feature you did not show at launch, or a just sold.
  • Any week you want to stay in front of your followers without posting a headshot.

A reel is weaker as a complete record of the home. Nobody picks a house from a reel; they decide whether to open the link.

What a reel is not

A reel is not a shortened property video. A 16:9 listing video cropped to vertical loses the edges of every room and keeps the slow pacing that works on a page and dies in a feed. The reel is built vertical from the start, from 9:16 material, with faster scenes.

It is also not a walkthrough, which is continuous footage, and not a story, which disappears in a day. The nearest confusable thing is a general agent branding reel, the kind with a trending sound and a pointing finger. A listing reel is about one address, and the address is the point.

A sixth-floor loft in 28 seconds

A photographer shoots a 1-bed loft with a river view on a Thursday and includes eight portrait frames alongside the landscape photos: the tall warehouse windows, the kitchen run from the doorway, the mezzanine stair, the bath, the sleeping area, the building entrance. The agent builds a 28-second reel on Friday. First scene is the river through the windows with the caption Sixth floor, facing the water. Then the kitchen, the stair, the sleeping area, the bath, the entrance, and the windows again to close, with the price and Link in bio on the last frame.

Captions run word by word under a recorded voiceover. The reel posts Friday evening with the branded page link in the bio. By Monday the enquiry form on the page has two requests for a Tuesday showing, and both sit under that listing in the app rather than in a comment thread.

Where reels fail

They open on the front door. They run forty seconds when the home has fifteen seconds of material. They have no captions, so the viewers scrolling in silence get nothing. They end without telling anyone what to do. Or they point to a portal page instead of a page you control, so the enquiry goes to whoever is advertising there.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

How long should a social reel for a listing be?

Long enough to show the home's best six to ten scenes and no longer, which is usually fifteen to forty seconds. Stop when the material runs out rather than padding to a round number.

Can I make a reel from the same photos as the listing video?

Only if some of those photos are portrait. A landscape photo cropped to vertical loses the sides of the room. Ask the photographer for a set of portrait frames alongside the landscape photos so the reel is built in 9:16 from the start.

Do listing reels need captions?

Yes. A large share of feed viewing happens with the sound off, and a reel without captions is silent to those viewers. Word-by-word captions keep the message readable and keep eyes on the screen.

Where should the link in a listing reel go?

To the property page you control, through the link in your bio or the platform's link feature. A page with your contact card and an enquiry form turns a viewer into a lead. A portal page may show other agents next to your listing.
