Shooting venues, restaurants and gyms: same kit, different shots

A real estate photographer can shoot venues, restaurants and gyms with the same kit. What changes is the shot list, the light and the page the client gets.

October 28, 2026 · 4 min read · by Rendprop

Short answer. The tripod, the wide lens and the level frame carry over from homes to venues, restaurants and gyms. What changes is the shot list: a venue needs capacity and the room set for an event, a restaurant needs the room at service light and the table a diner sits at, a gym needs the floor with and without people. The page each client needs changes too, from beds and baths to hours, capacity and membership.

A real estate photographer's kit shoots event venues, restaurants and gyms without a single new purchase. The tripod, the wide lens, the level frame and the path through the space all carry over. What changes is what the client's customer is deciding. A planner is deciding whether two hundred people fit. A diner is deciding whether to book a table. A prospective member is deciding whether they would feel comfortable on that floor at six in the evening. The shot list follows the decision, and the page you deliver changes its fields to match.

These are good clients for a photographer with a slow Tuesday. They rebook when the menu changes, when the room is redecorated, when a new class launches, and they do not have a seasonal listing cycle.

Event venues: capacity and the room set up

A planner wants to see the room empty, to judge the space, and the room set, to judge the event. Shoot both if you can arrange it. The shot list:

  • The main room empty, from the entrance, level, wide enough to read the size.
  • The same room set for a dinner or a ceremony, from the same spot, so the two frames compare.
  • The entrance and the approach, because guests arrive somewhere.
  • The bar, the terrace, the bridal or green room, the restrooms if they are good.
  • The view from the top table or the stage back toward the room.

Ask for the room's capacity in each configuration and put it on the page. The event venue post goes through what planners compare.

Restaurants: the room at service light

A restaurant photographed at eleven in the morning with the lights up looks like a canteen. Shoot it at the light it is sold under, which usually means late afternoon with the service lighting on and the daylight fading. The shot list:

  • The room from the door, at service light, tables set.
  • The table a diner would ask for: the window, the booth, the corner.
  • The bar from a stool.
  • The kitchen pass if the owner is proud of it.
  • Three or four dishes, shot at the table where they are served, not in a studio.
  • The exterior at dusk with the sign lit, which is the frame the owner will use everywhere.

Portrait frames of the dishes and the room make a reel the restaurant can post the same week, built the same way as a listing reel.

Gyms and studios: with and without people

An empty gym shows the equipment. A busy gym shows the atmosphere. A prospective member wants both, and the honest set gives them both. The shot list:

  • The main floor empty, early morning, cleaned, every machine straight.
  • The same floor during a class or a busy hour, with members' permission arranged by the owner.
  • Each distinct zone: free weights, cardio, the studio, the recovery area.
  • Changing rooms and showers, because they decide more memberships than the squat rack.
  • The front desk and the entrance.

The gym post covers the two visits in detail, including how to shoot people in a way that respects members.

The page changes with the mode

In Rendprop the mode chooser is set when you create the listing or workspace, with modes for real estate, event venues, restaurants and bars, gyms and studios, retail and other businesses such as offices and workshops. The fields, words and sample content change with the mode: a home shows price, beds, baths and square feet, while a business shows hours, menu, capacity or membership. The gallery, the video, the contact card and the enquiry form work the same way, and the enquiry form still asks for the date the visitor wants, which for a venue is the event date and for a gym is the day they want to come in. The industries page lists the modes, and the switching modes post covers running both kinds of client in one app.

Three business clients in one week

Monday, a wedding barn: empty room at 10:00, then back at 15:00 after the florist has set up a styled table, exterior at dusk. Capacity for seated dinner and standing reception from the owner. Page in event venue mode. Wednesday, a bistro: 16:30 arrival, room at service light, four dishes at the window table, exterior at 18:00 with the sign on. Page in restaurant mode with hours and the menu. Friday, a boxing gym: 06:00 empty floor, 18:00 class in progress with the owner's permission sorted, changing rooms in between. Page in gym mode with the membership details. Three clients, same kit, three pages, each with a branded link for the business and a video built from the frames.

What goes wrong when photographers cross over

  • Shooting a restaurant like a kitchen listing. Lights up, chairs on tables, no food. Shoot at service.
  • Empty-only gym sets. They look closed.
  • A venue with no sense of scale. Include something that gives size: a set table, a person at the far end, the room from the mezzanine.
  • AI edits on a business space without checking. Tidying a bar may remove a tap handle or a fixture the owner sells. Compare each edit with its original and keep the disclosure on.
  • Residential fields on a business page. Set the mode when you create the listing.

Next step: pick one restaurant and one gym you already know, offer each a shoot with a page and a reel, and build the shot lists above into your booking form as separate services.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

Do I need different equipment for venues, restaurants and gyms?

No. The tripod, wide lens and level frame carry over. A small continuous light helps in restaurants at service light, and a release process for photographing members helps in gyms, but the kit is the same.

What time of day should I shoot a restaurant?

At the light it is sold under, usually late afternoon into dusk with the service lighting on. Morning with the lights up makes the room look institutional.

How does the property page change for a business?

The mode chooser in Rendprop sets the fields. A venue, restaurant or gym page shows hours, menu, capacity or membership instead of beds and baths, with the same gallery, video, contact card and enquiry form.

Should a gym be photographed with people in it?

Both ways. An empty floor shows the equipment and a busy hour shows the atmosphere. Have the owner arrange permission from members before the busy session.
