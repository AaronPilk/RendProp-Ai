How to find your unbranded MLS link in Rendprop

After publishing, open the Share sheet and copy the unbranded link (rendprop.com/u/…): no name, no contact details, no branding, for the MLS tour field.

October 28, 2026 · 3 min read · by Rendprop

Short answer. Publish the listing, open the Share sheet, and copy the unbranded link. It looks like rendprop.com/u/ followed by the listing's slug and shows the property with no name, no contact details and no Rendprop branding, which is what the MLS virtual-tour field is for; check your own MLS rules. The branded link (rendprop.com/f/ plus the same slug) is the one for everywhere else.

Publish the listing, open the Share sheet, and copy the unbranded link. It has the form rendprop.com/u/ followed by the listing's slug, and shows the property with no name, no contact details and no Rendprop branding. That is the link for the MLS virtual-tour field.

The branded link, rendprop.com/f/ followed by the same slug, sits next to it in the same Share sheet and is the one for buyers, sellers, texts and social.

Why there are two links at all

An MLS virtual-tour field is generally not the place for your own marketing, because the tour gets syndicated and shown by other agents, and a normal property page with your contact card on it is the wrong thing to put there. That is why every published Rendprop listing gets two addresses to the same property: a branded page for your own marketing and an unbranded page for the MLS. What your MLS allows in that field is for its current rules to say. The two links section describes them, and the MLS rules post covers what the field usually expects. Check your own MLS rules; they vary.

Finding and copying the unbranded link

  1. Open the listing from Home. The listing has to be published; an unpublished listing has no links yet.
  2. Open the Share sheet.
  3. Choose the unbranded link and copy it. It starts rendprop.com/u/.
  4. Paste it into the MLS virtual-tour field (the field name varies by MLS; it may be called virtual tour, unbranded tour, or similar).
  5. Save the MLS entry and open the link from the MLS listing the way another agent would, to confirm it resolves and shows no branding.

What to check before you paste it

  • The link begins with /u/, not /f/. The /f/ link carries your contact card.
  • The page it opens shows the right property, the right main photo and the right price.
  • The video on the page is the one you reviewed, and it plays on request rather than autoplaying.
  • Any edited photos in the gallery carry their disclosure, and you have checked your own MLS rules on altered images.

A worked example: Tuesday night MLS entry

An agent publishes a 3-bed in a cul-de-sac at 8pm on Tuesday. From the Share sheet she copies both links into a note so she has them to hand. At her desk she fills in the MLS: photos, remarks, and in the virtual-tour field the rendprop.com/u/ link. She saves and opens the listing from the MLS as if she were a co-op agent. The page shows the home, the gallery and the video, with no name, no number, no branding. Good.

Then she pastes the /f/ link into the Saturday open house post and the text to the seller, because those are the places her name should be. Two links, two jobs, one listing. The branded and unbranded post goes through the division in more detail.

What the unbranded page shows and hides

Shows the property: the main photo, the details, the gallery and the selected listing video on request. Hides you: no name, no contact details, no Rendprop branding. Open the live link once after publishing so you know exactly what a co-op agent gets, and compare it with the branded link on your own phone.

Where this goes wrong

  • Pasting the branded link into the MLS. Easy to do when both are on the clipboard. Look for /u/ before you save.
  • Sending the unbranded link to a buyer. They get a page with no way to reach you except the form. Buyers get /f/.
  • Looking for the link before publishing. The links exist once the listing is published. Publish first.
  • Assuming every MLS accepts any link. Some have specific requirements for the field. Check your own MLS rules and the field's help text.
  • Forgetting to re-check after a change. If you update the price or mark the listing sold, open the MLS link again and see what it shows.

If the link is not where you expect

Confirm the listing is published and that you are signed in to the account that owns it. If it is published and you still cannot find the Share sheet, the support page is the place to ask.

Next step: open your most recent published listing, copy the rendprop.com/u/ link from the Share sheet, and put it in the MLS virtual-tour field tonight. Then open the MLS listing and check it as a co-op agent would.

FAQ

Questions on this topic

What does the unbranded link look like?

rendprop.com/u/ followed by the listing's slug. The branded link is rendprop.com/f/ followed by the same slug. Both point at the same listing.

Is the unbranded page a different listing?

No. Both links point at the same listing and the same slug; the unbranded one simply shows the property with no name, no contact details and no Rendprop branding. Open each once after publishing so you know what each audience sees.

Can I get the link before I publish?

No. The branded and unbranded links are created when the listing is published. Publish, then open the Share sheet.

Will the MLS accept the link?

The unbranded page is made for the virtual-tour field: no name, no contact details, no branding. MLS rules vary, so check your own MLS rules and the field's requirements before you rely on it.
