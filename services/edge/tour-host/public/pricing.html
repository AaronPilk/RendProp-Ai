Pricing questions

The details.

What counts as a tour render?

One walkthrough sent through Rendprop's cloud engine and re-rendered as a drone-smooth tour. Publishing the free on-device render, re-publishing and sharing the link are always free — a render is only counted when the engine runs on new footage.

Is there a free trial?

Seven days free on any plan, with the full plan unlocked — enough to take a real listing all the way through and judge the output on your own property. Apple grants the trial once per Apple ID across all Rendprop plans, so switching plans doesn't start a second one. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

On your device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rendprop. You can open the same sheet in the app under Settings → Plan & usage → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan keeps working until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my tours if I stop paying?

They stay. Cancelling or letting a plan lapse doesn't delete your listings, photos, reels or published tours, and links you've already sent keep working. What stops is the monthly allowance: you can't render new tours or run AI features until you subscribe again. If you want the content gone, you delete it — that's in the app too.

Do unused allowances roll over?

No. Tour renders reset with the calendar month; photo edits, reels and aerial intros refill on a rolling 30-day window. Either way nothing banks up.

What if I run out mid-month?

The app tells you before an allowance is spent and shows what's left under Settings → Plan & usage. If you need more, change plan in the app and Apple prorates the switch — or wait for the reset. There's nothing extra to buy on this website.

Why are the AI features metered at all?

Because every AI photo edit, reel and aerial intro is a real per-run cost on our side, and the expensive ones cost around a hundred times what a tour render does. Metering them separately is what lets the plans stay at $49–249 instead of $500. The numbers on this page are the exact numbers the app enforces — no asterisk, no silent throttling.

Which currency are the prices in?

The prices on this page are US dollars, and Rendprop launches on the US App Store. Every price you see inside the app comes from the App Store in your own currency, and that's what you're charged.

What does a real estate marketing agency cost instead?

Full-service marketing retainers for agents typically run $2,500–5,000 a month before ad spend, and per-listing media bought separately runs $635–1,450 per listing at typical U.S. rates as of August 2026. Rendprop's whole stack — tours, photos, reels, floor plans, listing pages and enquiries — is $49–249 a month across every listing. That gap is the product.
