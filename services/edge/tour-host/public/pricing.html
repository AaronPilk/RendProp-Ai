Pricing questions

The details.

What counts as a tour render?

One walkthrough sent through Rendprop's cloud AI engine and re-rendered as a drone-smooth tour. Publishing the free on-device render, re-publishing, and sharing the link are always free — renders are only counted when the AI engine runs on new footage.

Is there a free trial?

Seven days free, with enough AI included to take one real listing all the way through — a tour render, ten photo edits, a video clip, two AI aerial intros and one drone-glide upscale. That's deliberate: it's a whole finished listing to judge us on, including the features that actually differentiate us, not a watermarked sample. Nothing is charged until you confirm a plan, and there are no in-app purchases — plans are set up on rendprop.com (during early access, with the Rendprop team by email).

What if I need more in a month?

Add-on packs are available instead of jumping a tier — 50 extra AI photo edits for $10, 10 extra AI video clips for $15, or 5 extra AI aerial shots for $20. Drone-glide upscaling is a per-tour add-on from $19. Add-ons are arranged through rendprop.com, never inside the app. Tour renders themselves aren't sold separately; the plan allowances are generous because rendering is the cheap part.

Do unused allowances roll over?

No. Tour renders reset with the calendar month; photo edits, clips, and aerials refill on a rolling 30-day window. Either way nothing banks up. The AI behind photo edits, clips, and aerials costs real money per run, and letting months stack would either raise everyone's price or force us to throttle the people actually using it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, cancel any time through rendprop.com — plans are never managed inside the app — and your published tours stay live through your paid period. Deleting your account removes your content; that one is in the app, not behind a support ticket.

What does a real estate marketing agency cost instead?

Full-service marketing retainers for agents typically run $2,500–5,000 a month before ad spend, and per-listing media bought separately runs $635–1,450 per listing. Rendprop's whole stack — tours, photos, reels, floor plans, listing pages, and leads — is $49–249 a month across every listing. That gap is the product.

Why cap the AI features at all?

Because every AI photo edit, video clip, and aerial shot is a real per-run cost on our side, and the expensive ones cost 100× what a tour render does. Metering them separately is what lets the plans stay at $49–249 instead of $500. The numbers on this page are the exact numbers the app enforces — no asterisk, no silent throttling.

Are these prices final?

They're early-access rates. Founding members keep their rate as long as they stay subscribed, even when public pricing changes.
