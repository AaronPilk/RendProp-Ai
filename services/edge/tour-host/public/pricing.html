Pricing questions

The details.

What's the difference between the plans?

Every plan includes AI photo editing, photo-based listing and social videos, and hosted property websites with enquiries. Each step up includes more monthly AI usage, and Team adds three seats in one shared workspace. The app shows current plan features and your available usage before you create.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Eligible new subscribers in the U.S. start with Apple's seven-day introductory offer: you can prepare one draft listing, add photos and make up to five AI photo edits during the trial. Video generation and the optional AI Aerial opening are not included until paid access begins. The Aerial opening also requires an eligible allowance, available processing capacity and separate processor permission. Apple shows eligibility, renewal price and billing terms before confirmation. Downloading or signing in does not activate an offer.

How do I cancel?

On your device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rendprop. You can open the same sheet in the app under Settings → Plan & usage → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan keeps working until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my tours if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted when a plan ends. For subscriptions started on or after October 6, 2026, published tours stay live for 90 days after the subscription expires; we send notices before hosting ends, and renewing keeps them up. New renders and AI tools stop until you subscribe again. You can download your files from the app at any time, and delete content whenever you want.

Which currency are the prices in?

The prices on this page are U.S. prices in USD. Apple shows the price in your own currency in the app before confirmation, and that is what you are charged.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Change plans in the app under Settings → Plan & usage. Apple applies an upgrade right away and a downgrade at your next renewal. All plans are monthly; annual plans aren't offered right now.

What does a real estate marketing agency cost instead?

Typical U.S. rates for one listing's media package run $555–1,300 (see the math above). Rendprop plans start at $29.99 a month and cover photo editing, photo-based video, hosted listing pages and enquiries within your plan's monthly usage.
