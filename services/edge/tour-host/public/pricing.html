Pricing questions

The details.

What counts as a tour render?

One walkthrough sent through Rendprop's cloud AI engine and re-rendered as a drone-smooth tour. Publishing, re-publishing, and sharing the link are always free — renders are only counted when the AI engine runs on new footage.

Is there a free way to try it?

Early-access members get their first AI tour render free, so you can judge the output on your own listing before paying anything.

What if I need more tours in a month?

Add extra tour renders any time — $19 each on Solo, $15 on Pro, $12 on Team. Ultra 4K 60 fps rendering is available as a per-tour add-on for showcase properties.

Do unused renders roll over?

Photo edits roll over month to month while you're subscribed. Tour renders don't — they're what keeps the engine (and your price) sustainable.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, cancel in two taps inside the app, and your published tours stay live through your paid period. Deleting your account removes your content — that's in the app too, not behind a support ticket.

Are these prices final?

They're early-access rates. Founding members keep their rate as long as they stay subscribed, even when public pricing changes.
