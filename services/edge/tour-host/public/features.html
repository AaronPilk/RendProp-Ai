Scroll tours

A walkthrough they control

Your tour isn't a video people abandon after four seconds. It's a link they scrub with their thumb — forward, back, room by room — like scrolling a feed. That control is why they stay longer and remember the space.

  • ✓One link, everywhere. Text it, put it in the listing, drop it in bio. Viewers never need the app.
  • ✓Chapters per room so viewers jump straight to what they care about.
  • ✓Instant playback on any phone or laptop — video streams from the edge, no buffering wheel.
  • ✓Your branding on the page — name, photo, number, and a lead form. It's your page, not a portal's.

The drone-style render

Handheld in. Drone-smooth out.

This is the heart of the app. Your handheld walkthrough goes to Rendprop's cloud engine, which stabilizes the motion, smooths the pacing, and re-renders every frame — so the finished tour glides like it was flown, not walked.

  • ✓Up to 4K at 60 fps — a resolution photo-to-video apps don't reach.
  • ✓Real footage only. The engine enhances what your camera saw. It never invents rooms, windows, or furniture.
  • ✓Filmed at 0.5× wide — the app guides you to shoot the way the engine loves.
  • ✓Publish anyway, enhance later. In a rush? Skip AI enhance, share the on-device render, and upgrade the tour when it's done.

Social reels

From photos to post in minutes

Pick a few photos in the app and Rendprop animates each one into a gliding camera move, stitches them into a vertical reel, and adds the intro card and captions. Done before your coffee's cold.

  • ✓AI motion turns still photos into cinematic camera moves — pans, pushes, reveals.
  • ✓9:16 and 16:9 exports for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and MLS.
  • ✓Auto captions + address card baked in, so the reel sells even on mute.
  • ✓Animate any single photo into a motion clip for stories and covers.

Floor plans

Walk the rooms. Get the plan.

Buyers trust listings with floor plans — and almost nobody includes one because they cost $80–150 and take days. Rendprop turns a quick room scan into a clean 2D plan, right on the tour page.

  • ✓Scan with your iPhone — walk the rooms and the app maps walls, doors, and windows.
  • ✓Already have plans? Upload the blueprint (PDF or image) and skip the scan.
  • ✓Clean, labeled output — rooms, dimensions, furniture footprints, no clutter.

Leads & analytics

The tour works. You get the call.

Every tour page ends with your card and a lead form. Viewers request a showing right there, and the lead is delivered to you — not auctioned to three other agents.

  • ✓Lead form on every tour — name, contact, message, straight to you.

  • ✓Your leads stay yours. We don't resell them. That's the whole point.
