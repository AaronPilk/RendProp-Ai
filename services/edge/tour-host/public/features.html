Scroll tours

A walkthrough they control

Your tour isn't a video people abandon after four seconds. It's a link they scrub with their thumb — forward, back, room by room — like scrolling a feed. That control is why they stay longer and remember the space.

  • ✓One link, everywhere. Text it, put it in the listing, drop it in bio. Viewers never need the app.
  • ✓Chapters per room so viewers jump straight to what they care about.
  • ✓Fast playback on any phone or laptop — the video streams from the edge with a short pre-load, so once it starts the scrub never stutters.
  • ✓Your branding on the page — your name, your business, your number, your accent colour, and a contact form. It's your page, not a portal's.

The listing page

The link is the listing page

Below the flythrough, the same link keeps going: the details you typed into the app — price, beds, baths, and square feet for a home; hours, menu, capacity, membership, or the weekly special for a business — room chapters, a monthly-payment estimate for homes, your card, and a booking form. It builds itself from your listing: sections appear when you've filled them in and hide when you haven't. The live demo shows the layout fully dressed, with a story, gallery, and floor plan.

  • ✓A page per listing — the kind of single-property page that runs $9–150/month as a standalone tool.
  • ✓Contact form at the bottom, with the date they'd like, dropped straight into your inbox in the app.
  • ✓Payment estimate on home pages so buyers self-qualify before they call.
  • ✓Nothing to design or host. Publish from the app; the page is live at your link.

Two links, every time

One for buyers.
One for the MLS.

Publishing gives you both at once. You don't build the second one, and you can't forget it — it's made from the same tour.

The branded link

Your card, your accent colour, your contact form. This is the one you text, post and print on the flyer. Every enquiry from it lands in your inbox in the app, with the date they asked for.

The unbranded link

The same property, stripped: no name, no phone number, no contact form, no Rendprop branding, and no links off the page. Built for an MLS virtual-tour field, where branding can carry a fine.

Both from one Share sheet

Copy either link from the tour in the app. Your MLS decides what its own field allows, so check your rules before you post — the unbranded link is built to meet the strictest ones.

The drone-style render

Handheld in. Drone-smooth out.

This is the heart of the app. Your handheld walkthrough goes to Rendprop's cloud engine, which stabilizes the motion, smooths the pacing, and re-renders every frame — so the finished tour glides like it was flown, not walked.

  • ✓60 fps, every frame a keyframe — so the scroll-scrub lands on any frame instantly instead of jumping between them.
  • ✓Real footage only. The engine enhances what your camera saw. It never invents rooms, windows, or furniture.
  • ✓Filmed at 0.5× wide — the app guides you to shoot the way the engine loves.
  • ✓Publish anyway, enhance later. In a rush? Skip AI enhance, share the on-device render, and upgrade the tour when it's done.

Reels

From photos to post in minutes

Pick a few photos in the app and Rendprop animates each one into a gliding camera move, stitches them into a vertical reel, and puts your own voice over the top. Done before your coffee's cold.

  • ✓Your voiceover, or an AI voice — record straight into the app, or let it read your script.
  • ✓Word-by-word captions that land on the beat, so the reel sells even on mute.
  • ✓AI motion turns still photos into cinematic camera moves — pans, pushes, reveals.
  • ✓9:16 and 16:9 exports for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and the MLS.

Floor plans

Walk the rooms. Get the plan.

Buyers trust listings with floor plans — and almost nobody includes one because they cost $80–150 and take days. Rendprop turns a quick room scan into a clean 2D plan, right on the tour page.

  • ✓Scan with your iPhone — walk the rooms and the app maps walls, doors and windows. Scanning needs an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor.
  • ✓Already have plans? Upload the plan (PDF or image) and skip the scan — that works on every iPhone.
  • ✓Clean, labelled output — rooms, dimensions, furniture footprints, no clutter.
AI aerial intro · clearly disclosed

Aerial intros

Open like a film. Skip the drone.

Give the tour a cinematic establishing shot — a sweeping aerial-style approach AI-generated from your exterior photo, so it starts on the real building and flies out. It is an AI illustration, not real drone footage. No pilot, no permit, no weather window.

  • ✓4–8 second intros in widescreen or vertical, with a time of day and camera move you pick.
  • ✓Honest by design — the intro is disclosed as AI-generated, every time. Your buyers' trust stays intact.
  • ✓Optional. Purists can open on the real footage. Your call, per tour.
