How it works

Three steps. About ten minutes.

If you can walk through a property holding a phone, you can publish a cinematic tour today.

01

Film a walkthrough

One slow, steady pass on your iPhone with the wide lens. The app guides your route and pace — no equipment, no crew, no second takes.

02

AI makes it cinematic

Rendprop stabilizes, smooths, and re-renders your footage into a gliding, drone-style tour — up to 4K at 60 fps. Shaky hallway in, steadicam glide out.

03

Share one link

Your tour lives at one link people scroll through like social. Chapters, photos, floor plan, your card — and every lead lands in your inbox.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Do I need a drone or a gimbal?

No. One steady walkthrough on your iPhone is enough. Rendprop's AI smooths and re-renders the footage so the final tour glides like it was shot on a stabilized rig.

What do I film with?

Your iPhone. Use the wide lens, walk one slow pass through the space, and let the app guide you. Rendprop handles stabilization, motion smoothing, and rendering up to 4K at 60 fps.

Will the AI misrepresent my property?

No. Rendprop enhances what you filmed — it doesn't invent rooms or features. AI-edited photos are labeled, and synthetic aerial shots are clearly disclosed as illustrations. Your buyers' trust is the product; we don't spend it.

Who owns my footage and photos?

You do. Your content is never used for marketing or AI training without your written consent, and you can delete your account and all your content in the app at any time. The details live in our privacy policy.

When can I get the app?

Rendprop for iOS is in early access. Email aaron@pilk.ai and you'll get an invite as spots open.
