FAQ

Fair questions.

Is Rendprop an app?

Yes — Rendprop is an iPhone app, and it needs iOS 16 or later. You film, enhance, publish and read your enquiries on your phone. The people you send a tour to need nothing: it's a web link that opens anywhere.

Do I need a drone or a gimbal?

No. One steady walkthrough on your iPhone is enough. Rendprop smooths and re-renders the footage so the finished tour glides like it was shot on a stabilised rig.

Do I need an account to try it?

Rendprop is free to download, and everything except publishing works without an account — record, build, edit photos and preview your tour signed out. You sign in with Apple only when you publish, because that is the step that creates the hosted link and the contact form.

Will the AI misrepresent my property?

No. Rendprop enhances what you filmed — it doesn't invent rooms or features. Every AI photo edit is labelled “Virtually staged” on your tour and the untouched original is published right beside it, and the AI aerial intro is disclosed as AI-generated.

Can I use the tour in the MLS?

Yes. Every tour you publish gives you two links: a branded page with your card and contact form, and a separate unbranded link with no name, no contact details and no Rendprop branding — the one that's safe to paste into an MLS virtual-tour field. Check your own MLS rules before you post.

How is this different from AI reel makers?

Photo-to-video tools animate still photos into short clips. Rendprop starts from real footage, so nothing between the frames is invented — and it goes past the video: a hosted tour link, floor plans, a photo studio, reels and a contact form in one app. See the full comparison.

Is the tour link a real listing page?

Yes. Every published tour opens as its own page — the flythrough, then the details you entered in the app (price, beds, baths and square feet for a home; hours, menu, capacity or membership for a business), a payment estimate for homes, your card and a contact form. Nothing extra to build or pay for. Open the live demo — a fully dressed example with a story, gallery and floor plan — and scroll.

What does Rendprop cost?

Starter is $49 a month or $490 a year, Pro is $99 a month or $990 a year, and Team is $249 a month. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, available once per Apple ID. Prices are set by Apple and billed through the App Store, and you can cancel any time in Settings on your device. See what's in each plan.

Who owns my footage and photos?

You do. Your content is never used for our marketing or to train AI models without your written permission, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time. Details in our privacy policy.
