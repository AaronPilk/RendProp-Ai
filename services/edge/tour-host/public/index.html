FAQ

Fair questions.

Is Rendprop an app?

Yes — Rendprop is an iPhone app for property photos, photo-based videos and websites, and it needs iOS 16 or later. Add photos, review optional AI edits, create a video and publish from your phone. Buyers can open the published website in a browser.

Do I need a drone or a gimbal?

No. Start with landscape property photos for a listing video or choose portrait photos for social video. A recorded walkthrough is optional and is handled separately from photo-to-video creation. Aerial intros are Coming soon; new aerial creation is not available or included in any plan.

Do I need an account to try it?

You can record and prepare content locally before signing in. Publishing needs an internet connection, a connected workspace and an available allowance. Sign in with Apple enables account recovery, phone/Studio continuity and teams. Paid tools require a subscription or eligible Apple-confirmed trial.

Will the AI misrepresent my property?

AI can change or invent property details. Review every AI-edited photo and photo-based video against your original photos before publishing. AI-generated or edited media carries disclosures, and available original photos are linked for comparison. Aerial intros are Coming soon.

Can I use the tour in the MLS?

Yes. Every tour you publish gives you two links: a branded page with your card and contact form, and a separate unbranded link with no name, no contact details and no Rendprop branding — the one that's safe to paste into an MLS virtual-tour field. Check your own MLS rules before you post.

How is this different from AI reel makers?

Rendprop combines property photo editing, landscape listing videos, vertical social videos and a hosted property website with your gallery, selected video and contact form. Recorded walkthroughs are optional. Review AI-generated or edited media against your original photos before publication. See the full comparison.

Is the tour link a real listing page?

Yes. A published property website opens with your photos and property details. Its video player uses the listing video you selected for the website; recorded walkthroughs remain optional. The page can include the details you entered, a contact card and an enquiry form. Open the live demo to see a sample gallery, story and features list.

What does Rendprop cost?

Starter is $49 a month, Pro is $99 a month, and Team is $249 a month. Every plan bills monthly. Eligible subscribers can start a 7-day trial by confirming an Apple subscription in the app; installing or signing in does not start it. Prices are set by Apple and billed through the App Store, and you can cancel any time in Settings on your device. See what's in each plan.

Who owns my footage and photos?

You do. Rendprop never uses your content for its own marketing or AI training without your written permission. Optional Kie photo-to-video processing asks for separate permission before selected photos are sent. You can request account deletion in the app; provider-held copies and shared-workspace records have separate retention limits. Details in our privacy policy.
