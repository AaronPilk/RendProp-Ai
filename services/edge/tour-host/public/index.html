FAQ

Fair questions.

Is Rendprop an app?

Yes — Rendprop is a native iOS app. You film, enhance, publish, and track everything from your iPhone. Viewers don't need the app: your tour is a web link that opens anywhere.

Do I need a drone or a gimbal?

No. One steady walkthrough on your iPhone is enough. Rendprop's AI smooths and re-renders the footage so the final tour glides like it was shot on a stabilized rig.

Will the AI misrepresent my property?

No. Rendprop enhances what you filmed — it doesn't invent rooms or features. AI-edited photos are labeled, and synthetic aerial shots are clearly disclosed as illustrations. Your buyers' trust is the product; we don't spend it.

How is this different from AI reel makers?

Photo-to-video tools animate still photos into short clips. Rendprop starts from real footage, so nothing is invented — and it goes past the video: hosted tour link, floor plans, photo studio, reels, and lead capture in one app. See the full comparison.

Is the tour link a real listing page?

Yes. Every published tour opens as its own page — the flythrough, then the listing details you entered in the app (price, beds, baths, and square feet for a home; hours, menu, capacity, membership and the like for a business), a payment estimate for homes, your card, and a booking form that captures leads. Nothing extra to build or pay for. Open the live demo — a fully dressed example with a story, gallery, and floor plan — and scroll.

What does listing marketing normally cost?

Bought separately in the U.S.: photos $150–300, a walkthrough video $250–600, drone $100–350, a 3D tour $200–400, virtual staging $15–50 a photo, and a single-property site $9–150 a month. Full-service marketing agencies charge $2,500–5,000 a month in retainers. Rendprop's plans run $49–249 a month for all of it, on every listing. See the math.

Who owns my footage and photos?

You do. Your content is never used for marketing or AI training without your written consent, and you can delete your account and all your content in the app at any time. Details in our privacy policy.
