Features/The listing website

A website for every listing.
Nothing to design or host.

A single property website is one page for one listing: the photos, the video, the price and details, your contact card and an enquiry form, at its own link. Rendprop builds that page from the listing you already made in the app. Choose the photos and the video, check the details, and publish.

Updated November 12, 2026 · by Rendprop

How it works

From listing to live page in three steps.

01

Build the listing

Create a listing in the app and add your photos and details: price, beds, baths and square feet for a home.

02

Pick what it shows

Choose the main photo, the gallery and the listing video, then check your contact card.

03

Publish and share

Publish from the app. You get a branded link for buyers and an unbranded link for the MLS.

Short answer. Every Rendprop plan includes a hosted property website for each listing you publish. The page shows your photos, the listing video you chose, the details you entered, your contact card and an enquiry form, and enquiries land in the app. Plans start at $29.99 a month.

What's on the page

  • Your main photo first. The page opens with the main photo you selected, and your gallery.
  • The listing video, on request. The video you chose plays when a visitor taps it. It doesn't autoplay.
  • The details you typed into the app. Price, beds, baths and square feet for a home. Each section appears when you've filled it in and stays hidden when you haven't, so there are no empty boxes.
  • Your contact card. Your name, your business, your number and your accent colour, with one tap to reach you.
  • An enquiry form. It asks for a name, phone, email and the date they'd like.

Open the live demo listing to see the layout filled in, with a story, gallery and features list. It's a made-up example property, not a home for sale.

Two links: one for buyers, one for the MLS

Publishing gives you two links at once. The branded link carries your card and the enquiry form. That's the one you text, post and print on the flyer. The unbranded link shows the same property with no name, no phone number, no contact form, no Rendprop branding and no links off the page, which is what an MLS virtual tour field asks for. Copy either one from the Share sheet. MLS rules differ by market, so check yours before you post.

Where enquiries go

When a visitor fills in the form, the enquiry lands in your inbox in the app with the date they asked for, listed per listing and all together. Rendprop doesn't resell your enquiries.

Photographers can show a client's contact details on a listing they manage. Sending enquiries on to that client needs the client to verify their email address first.

Photos and video, ready before you publish

The page uses what you already made in the app. Edit the photos first if they need it, with a blue sky, a twilight exterior or virtual staging, each saved as a labelled version (photo editing and staging). Then make the listing video from the photos you picked (listing video from photos) and choose it for the page.

After the sale, or if you stop paying

Mark a listing sold or archived in the app and the page says so, then offers to help visitors find something similar.

Nothing is deleted when a plan ends. For subscriptions started on or after October 6, 2026, published pages stay live for 90 days after the subscription expires. Rendprop sends notices before hosting ends, and renewing keeps them up.

Search engines

Whether search engines index a listing page depends on its owner's setting. Until it's allowed, the page asks search engines not to index it, because it carries your name, your number and the property address. AI crawlers are kept off customer pages either way.

What it costs

The property website is part of every plan, alongside AI photo editing and video from photos. In the U.S. the plans are Pro $29.99, Max $49, Ultra $99.99 and Team $249.99 a month, with three seats on Team. Publishing needs an available allowance on your plan, and the app shows your usage before you create.

Website publishing starts with the first paid month. The 7-day free trial for eligible new U.S. subscribers covers one draft listing, photos and up to five AI photo edits. Apple shows the price and offer terms before you confirm.

For comparison, standalone single-property website tools typically run $9 to $150 a month (typical U.S. rates as of August 2026). See plans and the free trial.

Why have your own page

Portal pages belong to the portal. A single property website is yours: one listing, your card, your enquiry form, and a link you control for the sign, the email and the post. What a single property website should include goes through the parts that matter.

FAQ

Questions about property websites.

What is a single property website?

A web page for one listing and nothing else: the photos, the video, the price and details, a contact card and an enquiry form, at its own link. Rendprop publishes one for each listing from the iPhone app.

Do I need my own domain or web hosting?

No. Rendprop hosts the page at a rendprop.com link, so there's no domain or hosting to set up.

Which link goes in the MLS?

The unbranded link. It shows the property with no name, contact details, contact form or Rendprop branding. Copy it from the Share sheet, and check your MLS rules first.

Where do enquiries go?

To your inbox in the app, per listing and all together, with the date the visitor asked for. Rendprop doesn't resell them.

What happens after the home sells?

Mark it sold or archived in the app. The page says so and offers to help visitors find something similar.

Will the page show up on Google?

Only if you allow it. Search indexing of a customer listing depends on its owner's setting, and until then the page asks search engines not to index it. Allowing it does not guarantee a ranking.
