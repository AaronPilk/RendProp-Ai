Features/Photo-based videos

Turn your listing photos
into a listing video.

You don't need a videographer or a drone. Add your property photos to a listing in Rendprop, choose a landscape listing video or a vertical social video, and the app plans the scene order and camera motion. Review it, add your voice if you want, then download it or put it on the listing's property website.

Updated November 12, 2026 · by Rendprop

How it works

Photos in. Video out. You check it.

01

Pick your photos

Add photos to a listing. If you made AI edits, compare them with your originals and choose which saved versions go into the video.

02

Choose the format

A landscape video for the listing website, YouTube and the MLS, or a vertical video for Instagram and TikTok. Scene order and camera motion are planned for you.

03

Review, then share

Add your own voice or an AI voice if you want. Check the finished video, then download it or set it as the listing video on the property website.

Short answer. Rendprop makes a landscape listing video from your landscape photos and a vertical social video from your portrait photos. The app plans the scene order and the camera moves, voiceover and music are optional, and you review the result before anyone sees it. Video is included with every paid plan, from $29.99 a month.

What you need

  • Property photos. Landscape photos make the listing video. Portrait photos make the vertical social video.
  • An iPhone on iOS 16 or later. Rendprop is an iPhone app, with a companion web Studio at studio.rendprop.com.
  • A paid plan. Video starts with the first paid month. The free trial covers photos and AI photo edits, not video.

You don't need a drone, a gimbal or a videographer. A recorded walkthrough is optional and separate from making a video from photos.

Voice, captions and music

All of the sound is optional. Record your own voiceover straight into the app, or have an AI voice read a script you write. Captions appear word by word, so the video still makes sense to people scrolling with the sound off. Music is your call. If you plan to record yourself, this guide to recording on an iPhone covers the basics.

The motion is generated, so check it

A video made from photos is not a recording. The pans, pushes and reveals are generated from still images, so a crop can land badly or a detail can change. AI can change or invent property details. Watch every video against your original photos before you post it, and drop anything that misrepresents the home. Here is a short checklist for that review.

Rendprop labels AI-generated and AI-edited media and keeps your original photos. Photo-to-video processing by Kie is optional, and the app asks for your permission separately before any selected photos are sent.

Get the photos right first

The video can only show what the photos show. Rendprop's photo editing gives you a blue sky, a twilight exterior, a green lawn, a tidier room or furniture in an empty room. Each edit is saved as a new, labelled version, and you choose which versions go into the video. See photo editing and virtual staging for how that works.

Where the video goes next

Download it and post it anywhere, or choose it as the listing video on the property website Rendprop publishes for the listing. That page holds your gallery, the details you entered, your contact card and an enquiry form, and the video plays when a visitor asks for it instead of autoplaying. Publishing also gives you an unbranded link, with no name, contact details or Rendprop branding, for the MLS virtual tour field. Single property websites explains the page, or you can open the live demo listing.

What it costs

Video from photos is included with every paid plan. In the U.S. the plans are Pro $29.99, Max $49, Ultra $99.99 and Team $249.99 a month, with three seats on Team. Each step up includes more monthly AI usage, and the app shows what you have available before you create. Plans are bought through Apple, which shows the price and any introductory offer before you confirm.

Eligible new subscribers in the U.S. can start with a 7-day free trial. It covers one draft listing, your photos and up to five AI photo edits. Video starts with the first paid month.

For comparison, typical U.S. rates as of August 2026 were $250 to $500 for a videographer's walkthrough video and $75 to $150 for a social cutdown, for each listing. See plans and the free trial.

When to film a walkthrough instead

Photo video works when you already have good photos and want something to post the same day. A recorded walkthrough is different: the camera's actual path provides the movement, so viewers see what the camera captured. Rendprop handles both, and the walkthrough stays optional. When to film the home yourself goes through the trade-off.

FAQ

Questions about video from photos.

Can I make a listing video from photos only?

Yes. Rendprop makes a landscape listing video from your landscape photos and a vertical social video from your portrait photos. No recorded footage is needed; a walkthrough is optional.

Do I need a drone, a gimbal or a videographer?

No. You need property photos and an iPhone on iOS 16 or later. The camera motion is generated from your photos, so review the video before you post it.

Is video included in the free trial?

No. The 7-day free trial for eligible new U.S. subscribers covers one draft listing, photos and up to five AI photo edits. Video and website publishing begin with the first paid month.

Can I add my own voiceover?

Yes. Record it straight into the app, or have an AI voice read your script. Captions are added word by word, and music is optional.

Can I use staged or edited photos in the video?

Yes. Choose any saved version, including reviewed staged or decluttered photos. Staged photos are labelled Virtually staged, other AI edits carry a disclosure, and your original stays in photo history.

Can the video go on the MLS?

The 16:9 export is made for places like YouTube and the MLS, and every published listing also gets an unbranded link for the MLS virtual tour field. MLS rules differ, so check yours before you post.
