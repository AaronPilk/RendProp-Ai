The honest comparison

Make the video.
Then share the listing.

AutoReel, Mirino, Arvaum and Rendy offer useful ways to make marketing media from photos. Rendprop makes videos from photos too, can use recorded walkthrough footage, and publishes a property website with your selected video, gallery and contact form. Here's how the workflows compare, based on each product's own site as checked on 26 August 2026.

The short version

Three workflow differences worth comparing.

Photos or footage in

Choose saved photos for a short video, or use recorded walkthrough footage. Photo motion creates movement from stills; a recorded walkthrough shows what the camera captured. Review either result before sharing it.

iPhone app and Studio

Rendprop has an iPhone app for work at the property and a companion web Studio. Capture or import media, review the result, publish the listing and follow enquiries in the same workflow.

A page as well as a video

Download the video or share a branded property page with your selected video, gallery and contact form. An unbranded link is available for the MLS.

AutoReel

The category incumbent. Web-based photo-to-reel with templates, a real timeline editor, voiceover and avatars, photo edits, and listing import. Polished and mature — if a templated 60–75 second reel is all you need, it delivers.

Why it matters

Choose the source that fits the home.

With only photos, Rendprop can turn selected images into a short video. The motion is generated from stills, so check the crop and finished clip. With a recorded walkthrough, the camera's actual path provides the movement. Either way, the selected video can lead a hosted property website alongside the photo gallery and contact form.

Straight answer

Who should pick what.

  • ✓Only need templated reels from MLS photos? AutoReel or Mirino will do the job; Rendy if you're a photographer selling to agents.
  • ✓Only need photo edits at a desk? Arvaum is a fair tool for that workflow.
  • ✓Want photos, video and a property website in one workflow? Rendprop lets you make a video from selected photos or use recorded footage, then share a branded listing page with a contact form and an unbranded MLS link.
