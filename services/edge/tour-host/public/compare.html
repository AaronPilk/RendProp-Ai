The honest comparison

They animate photos.
We film the real thing.

AutoReel, Mirino, Arvaum and Rendy are capable tools — and every one of them starts from still photos, on a website or a desktop app. Rendprop starts from a real walkthrough on your iPhone, and doesn't stop at a video file. Here's the full picture, strengths and all — checked against each product's own site on 26 August 2026.

The short version

Three differences that decide it.

Real footage in

Photo-to-video AI has to guess what's between the photos — motion that never happened. Rendprop's engine enhances footage your camera actually captured. Nothing around the corner is invented, because you walked around the corner.

An app, not a website

Every tool here runs in a browser or on a desktop. Rendprop is an iPhone app — you film, enhance, publish, and read your enquiries on the phone that's already in your hand at the property.

A link, not a file

Their output is a video file you post and hope. Rendprop's output is a hosted scroll tour with chapters, photos, a floor plan, your card and a contact form — plus an unbranded twin for the MLS. The marketing and the follow-up live at one link.

Why it matters

AI guessing vs. AI enhancing.

When AI animates a still photo, it has to invent every frame between the two it was given — which is why tools in that category publish guidance on spotting and re-rolling odd details, and why their reviewers mention them. It's structural, not a bug that patches out. When AI enhances real footage it has the truth on every frame, and only makes it smoother, sharper and steadier. That's Rendprop's entire architecture — and it's why a Rendprop tour can be scrubbed frame by frame at 60 fps while photo-based tools hand you a linear clip.
