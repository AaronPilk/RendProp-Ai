They animate photos.
We film the real thing.

AutoReel, Mirino, Arvaum, and Rendy are capable tools — and every one of them starts from still photos on a website or desktop app. Rendprop starts from a real walkthrough on your iPhone, and doesn't stop at a video file. Here's the full picture, strengths and all.

Real footage in

Photo-to-video AI has to guess what's between the photos — motion that never happened. Rendprop's engine enhances footage your camera actually captured. Nothing around the corner is invented, because you walked around the corner.

An app, not a website

Every tool here runs in a browser or on a desktop. Rendprop is a native iOS app — you film, enhance, publish, and read your leads on the phone that's already in your hand at the property.

A link, not a file

Their output is an MP4 you post and hope. Rendprop's output is a hosted scroll tour with chapters, photos, a floor plan, your card, and a lead form. The marketing and the follow-up live at one URL.

Why it matters

AI guessing vs. AI enhancing.

When AI animates a still photo, it must invent every frame between — that's where phantom doors and warped ceiling fans come from, and it's structural, not a bug that patches out. When AI enhances real footage, it has the truth on every frame and just makes it smoother, sharper, and steadier. That's Rendprop's entire architecture — and it's why we can offer 4K at 60 fps while photo-based tools cap at 1080p.
