The local editing preview

Build the sequence.
Keep the story yours.

Start with video and photos you already have. These are the preview's editing tools, not a promise of automatic production, cloud rendering or social publishing.

01 · Bring your media

Video and photos together

Import local clips and images into one sequence. Choose the shots that explain the space, then arrange them in the order you want viewers to experience.

02 · Shape the cut

Trim and reorder

Choose each clip's start and end, reorder the sequence and scrub playback to review the cut. Your original files stay the source of the story.

03 · Frame the message

Format and text

Prepare a vertical 9:16, landscape 16:9 or square 1:1 composition. Add text overlays for the details you want to call out. Check how each crop treats important features.

04 · Take it with you

Local export

Export in a format your browser supports. A downloaded video is separate from publishing a Rendprop tour; review the file before sharing it through your own channels.

Account continuity

One workspace.
Not another media silo.

The connected experience is being built around your existing Rendprop identity, workspace, listings and media. It is not a second set of accounts or a second subscription.

What is local, and what is connected?

  • Local preview: media you select, editing drafts and content plans in this browser.
  • Connected rollout: sign in to read the workspace and listing media you already own.
  • Not implied: automatic draft sync, cloud backup, a new AI generation service or one-click social publishing.

Keep your source files. Clearing browser storage can remove local drafts; reopening a draft may require selecting its media again.

Clear expectations

Before you open the preview.

Is the browser studio available yet?

Studio is a preview in rollout. The public editor destination and connected-workspace sign-in are not announced as generally available here. The iPhone app remains available from the App Store. Contact support for current preview access.

Does Studio replace the iPhone app?

No. The iPhone app handles guided camera capture and supported room scanning. The browser preview focuses on editing existing video and photos. A browser cannot supply the app's AR or LiDAR capture.

Can I open my existing Rendprop workspace?

Connected workspace access is being prepared for the same Rendprop account and workspace. It requires the browser sign-in configuration and read service to be released. Local preview projects are not automatically synced to the iPhone app.

Does the content calendar post to social accounts?

No. The preview calendar is for local planning, caption drafts and a calendar-file download. It does not connect to social accounts, schedule a social upload or publish on your behalf.

What should I know about exports and drafts?

Local export depends on the recording formats supported by your browser. The editor must show the available format rather than promise MP4 everywhere. Keep your source files: a saved local draft may ask you to select its media again, and local drafts are not a cloud backup.

Is there a separate Studio subscription?

No separate web checkout or Studio plan is being introduced in this preview. Rendprop's existing subscriptions remain billed through Apple. See the pricing page for the unchanged US plans and usage allowances.
