Features/AI Photo Studio

Edit and stage listing photos
on your iPhone.

Rendprop is a real estate photo editing app for iPhone. One tap gives you a blue sky, a twilight exterior, a green lawn, a tidier room or furniture in an empty room, and you can type your own edit. Every edit is saved as a new, labelled version, and your original stays in photo history.

Updated November 12, 2026 · by Rendprop

How it works

Three steps. Your original stays in photo history.

01

Add the photos

Add your listing photos to a listing in the app.

02

Tap an edit, or type one

Blue sky, twilight, green lawn, tidy the room, add furniture, or a custom prompt. Tap Suggest and the app proposes edits, with a reason for each.

03

Compare and choose

Check each edit against the original, then choose which versions go into the gallery and the video.

Short answer. Rendprop edits listing photos on your iPhone with one-tap edits (blue sky, twilight, green lawn, tidy the room and add furniture) and a custom prompt box. Staged photos are labelled Virtually staged, other edits carry an AI-alteration disclosure, and your original stays in photo history. Photo editing is in every plan, from $29.99 a month.

Type the edit you want

Not every photo fits a preset. Type one sentence, such as "clear the counters" or "make it golden hour", and the app's AI turns it into a precise edit brief you can change before it runs. "Improve my prompt" tightens your wording with one tap. Not sure what a photo needs? Tap Suggest and the app looks at the shot and proposes the edits worth making, with a reason for each.

Labels and originals

Every edit is a separate saved version. Staging is labelled Virtually staged, and other edits carry an AI-alteration disclosure. Your untouched original stays in photo history, and the edit history keeps the original, decluttered and staged versions together. When you export, you choose the versions, and AI exports include the matching disclosure.

Each listing also has a Compliance section that lists every AI asset with its disclosure and can export the audit log. Any disclosure rules your MLS or local advertising rules add on top are yours to meet; the app gives you the record to do it with.

Review every edit before it goes live

AI can change or invent property details, and results vary. A twilight sky is fine; a twilight edit that also adds a window is not. Compare each edit with the original before you publish it, and leave out anything a buyer would notice at the showing. Five photo edits buyers notice at the showing covers the usual problems, and virtual staging rules covers labelling.

From edited photos to a video and a website

Edited photos don't have to sit in a folder. Choose the reviewed versions, staged or decluttered included, and Rendprop turns them into a landscape listing video or a vertical social video, then puts the photos and the video on the listing's property website. See listing video from photos and single property websites.

Your photos stay yours

You own your photos. Rendprop never uses your content for its own marketing or for AI training without your written permission. The details are in the privacy policy.

What it costs

AI photo editing is in every plan: sky, lawn, twilight, declutter, virtual staging and custom edits. In the U.S. the plans are Pro $29.99, Max $49, Ultra $99.99 and Team $249.99 a month, with three seats on Team. Each step up includes more monthly AI usage, and the app shows your available usage before you create.

Eligible new subscribers in the U.S. can start with a 7-day free trial that includes up to five AI photo edits on one draft listing. Apple shows the price and offer terms before you confirm.

For comparison, typical U.S. rates as of August 2026 were $5 to $15 a photo for virtual staging and about $5 to $25 for each twilight conversion from outsourcing services. See plans and the free trial.

Phone or desk?

If you edit at a desk with local files, a desktop editor such as Arvaum Studio is a fair choice for that workflow. Rendprop is built for editing on the phone and carrying the photos straight into a video and a listing page. Rendprop vs Arvaum Studio compares the two.

FAQ

Questions about photo editing.

What edits can the app make?

One-tap blue sky, twilight, green lawn, tidy the room (declutter) and add furniture (virtual staging), plus a custom prompt box where you type the edit you want. Suggest proposes edits for a photo, with a reason for each.

Is virtual staging allowed in listing photos?

Rules differ by MLS and market, so check yours. Rendprop labels staged photos Virtually staged, keeps the original in photo history, and lists every AI asset with its disclosure in the listing's Compliance section.

Will the AI change the property?

It can. AI can change or invent property details, and results vary. Compare every edit with your original before you publish it.

How many edits do I get?

Each plan includes monthly AI usage, and each step up includes more. The app shows your available usage before you create. The 7-day free trial for eligible new U.S. subscribers includes up to five AI photo edits.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone on iOS 16 or later and an Apple ID. You sign in with Apple to create your account.

Who owns the edited photos?

You do. Rendprop never uses your content for its own marketing or for AI training without your written permission.
