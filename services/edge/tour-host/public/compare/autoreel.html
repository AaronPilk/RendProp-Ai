What AutoReel is

AutoReel (autoreelapp.com) is a web-based tool, founded in 2023 and based in the U.S., that turns listing photos into AI-animated video. Each photo becomes a short animated clip, and the clips are stitched into a reel. Around that core: templates, captions, music, AI voiceover, AI photo edits and virtual staging with a disclaimer stamp, and a timeline-style editor they call Studio, where you reorder, trim and adjust. You can import photos from listing sites, export the finished video branded or unbranded, and schedule it to social accounts. There are programs for photographers and realtors, an API for enterprise users, and a large help centre and blog.

Two things to know. There is no iPhone app listed, so the work happens in a browser. And the product ends at the video, a file or a scheduled post; nothing in it hosts the listing or collects a buyer's details.

Where AutoReel is the better pick

If the reel itself is the product you are buying, AutoReel is built for that and has been refining it since 2023. Pick it when one of these is you.

  • You want hands-on control of the edit. A marketing coordinator cutting ten reels on a Friday afternoon wants to swap clip order, trim a clip that runs long and re-time a caption. That is timeline-editor work, and AutoReel has a real one. Rendprop plans scene order and camera motion automatically; you choose the inputs and review the result, but you do not scrub a timeline.
  • You need scheduling or autoposting. AutoReel schedules the finished reel to your social accounts. Rendprop does not; you export the 9:16 or 16:9 file and post it yourself, or share the listing link.
  • You pull photos straight from listing sites. AutoReel imports from listing pages. In Rendprop you add photos from your iPhone or through the web Studio.
  • You want their photographer program, their realtor program or an API. All three are AutoReel features. Rendprop's photographer path is different: you present a client's contact details on the listing page, with enquiry forwarding once the client's email is verified.

Where Rendprop is the better pick

Rendprop wins when the question is not how to get a reel but what to send the buyer, and where the enquiry lands.

  1. The output is a listing page with a link, not only a video file. Take a 3-bed on a corner lot listed on a Tuesday. The buyer taps the link from a text, plays the video, scrolls the gallery, reads the price, beds, baths and square feet, and sends an enquiry with the date they want to visit. See the listing site.
  2. Two links per listing. A branded page at rendprop.com/f/your-slug and an unbranded page at rendprop.com/u/your-slug with no name, contact details or Rendprop branding, built for an MLS virtual-tour field. AutoReel's unbranded video export solves the file problem; it is not an unbranded tour page for that field. Check your MLS's current rules either way. See two links.
  3. Enquiries land in the app. The page carries a contact card and an enquiry form; submissions arrive in the Rendprop inbox, per listing and all together. A reel leaves the buyer to find you.
  4. It is an iPhone app. Add the photos, review the edits, create the video and publish from the phone in the car after the shoot, then pick up on the desktop in the web Studio. AutoReel lists no iPhone app.
  5. Review-first AI. Both tools stage rooms and both label it, which is to AutoReel's credit. Rendprop's difference is the review step: every AI edit is a separate version beside the original, staging is labelled "Virtually staged", other edits carry an AI-alteration disclosure, and the untouched original stays in photo history. You compare and choose before anything publishes.
  6. Beyond homes. Event venue, restaurant and bar, gym and studio, retail and office modes change the fields, the words and the page. A restaurant page asks for hours and menu, not beds and baths. AutoReel is described around listings.
  7. An optional recorded walkthrough. Handheld footage of the real home, not drone, that viewers can watch or scroll, separate from the photo-based video.

Workflow, side by side

Same listing, 24 photos from a Saturday shoot, two paths.

In AutoReel

Open the browser. Upload the photos or import them from the listing site. Choose a template. AutoReel animates each photo into a clip and stitches the clips into a reel. Add captions, music or an AI voiceover. Open their Studio editor to reorder, trim and adjust. Export branded or unbranded, then schedule the post or download the file. The reel goes to social; the listing lives wherever it already did.

In Rendprop

Open the app and start a listing in real estate mode. Add landscape photos for the listing video and portrait photos for the social video. In the photo studio, review optional AI edits, compare each against the original and pick the versions to use. Create the video: scene order and camera motion are planned automatically, you record a voiceover or let an AI voice read your script, with word-by-word captions, optional music and 9:16 and 16:9 exports. Review it, publish the property website, and from the Share sheet copy the branded link for buyers and the unbranded link for the MLS. When a buyer fills in the form, the enquiry is in your inbox.

The honest summary: AutoReel ends with a better-controlled reel. Rendprop ends with a reel plus a page, two links and an inbox, with less control over the cut.

Who should choose which

  • Choose AutoReel if you want finished reels with templates and a timeline editor on a desktop, need scheduling or autoposting, import photos from listing sites, or need an API.
  • Choose Rendprop if what you hand the buyer is a link, you need an unbranded page for the MLS tour field, you want enquiries in one inbox, you work from your iPhone, or you also market venues, restaurants, gyms or retail spaces.
  • Use both if your reels need scheduling and your listings need a page. The photos are the same either way.

FAQ

AutoReel or Rendprop: common questions

Does AutoReel publish a property website?

No. As of their public site in October 2026, AutoReel's deliverable is a video file, branded or unbranded, plus social posting. Rendprop publishes a hosted page for each listing with the video, the gallery, the property details, a contact card and an enquiry form.

Can I use an AutoReel video in the MLS?

Check your MLS's current rules before posting any video or link. AutoReel exports unbranded video files. Rendprop gives each listing an unbranded page link with no name, contact details or branding, built for the virtual-tour field.

Does Rendprop have a timeline editor like AutoReel's Studio?

No. Rendprop plans scene order and camera motion automatically; you choose the photos, the AI edits, the voiceover, the captions and the music, then review the video before publishing. If you want to trim clips and move them around on a timeline, AutoReel is the better fit.

Can Rendprop schedule posts to Instagram or Facebook?

No. Rendprop gives you 9:16 and 16:9 exports to post yourself, and a listing link to share. AutoReel includes social scheduling, so if autoposting is a requirement, that is a point for AutoReel.

How do the two handle virtual staging?

Both label it. AutoReel stamps staged images with a disclaimer. Rendprop labels staging as Virtually staged, keeps every AI edit as a separate version beside the original, and keeps the untouched original in photo history. In either tool, compare the edited photo against your original and check your MLS rules before publishing.

Can I use AutoReel and Rendprop together?

Yes. Use AutoReel for the scheduled reels and Rendprop for the hosted listing page, the unbranded MLS link and the enquiry inbox. The photos are the same either way, so the extra work is small.
