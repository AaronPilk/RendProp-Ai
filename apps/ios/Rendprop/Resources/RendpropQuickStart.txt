Welcome to Rendprop. Turn your property photos into polished marketing, a professional video, and a beautiful listing website.

Sign in with Apple, choose a plan, and confirm Apple’s offer and renewal price. Eligible accounts get seven days free with usage limits. Cancel through Apple anytime.

Tap Get started. Add the address, property details and buyer contact. Take or upload clear, high-resolution photos. Shoot landscape for a listing or website. Keep the phone level and use natural light. Indoors, use the point-five lens if available and needed to fit the room. Avoid extreme angles.

A recorded walkthrough is optional. Walk slowly and steadily, turning smoothly around corners. A gimbal can help. Tag rooms adds chapter jumps; Re-render refreshes your recorded tour.

In Photos, start from Original. Import brightens your photos automatically. Tap the wand to declutter, stage or make another edit. Choose your staging style. Compare every result with its source, check windows, walls and fixtures, then choose your gallery versions and cover. Your originals stay unchanged. Decluttered and Staged keep saved results; start another style from Original.

Tap Make a video. Choose Listing for landscape or Social media for vertical, then select reviewed photos. Rendprop plans the order and varied, restrained motion. Each photo becomes its own shot. Check the framing preview before creating. Under Optional, add voice, music or labels.

After Rendprop confirms your video is processing, you can leave the app. Turn on notifications to be told when it is ready. Review the finished video privately before publishing.

Publish your included Classic website, or preview the other designs. Buyers can explore Home, Gallery and Location, watch the video, and request information or a showing.

Share the website link; buyers don't need the app. Send your coordinator a photo ZIP with Originals, Decluttered and Staged folders plus listing or social copies. Check your MLS's requirements before uploading.

Find enquiries grouped by property in Leads. Need a hand? Tap Ask AI for help on your current screen.
