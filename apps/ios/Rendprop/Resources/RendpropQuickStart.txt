Rendprop — Complete first-listing guide
ElevenLabs Faye narration · real app screens

FIND YOUR WAY

01. Your first listing, from start to share
Welcome to Rendprop. This guide walks through one complete listing: enter the home, capture photos, review optional edits, make a video, publish the website, and follow up with buyers.
On screen: One home keeps the work together • You can leave while AI work continues • Review every result before sharing

02. Five tabs keep work organized
The bottom bar has Home, Listings, Activity, Leads, and Account. Home starts new work. Listings holds each property. Activity tracks jobs. Leads holds enquiries. Account contains your profile, settings, and plan.
On screen: Home → start • Listings → property files • Activity, Leads, Account → follow through

03. Sign in, then explore
Sign in with Apple and finish the short introduction. Exploring the app does not start a free trial. A trial begins only if Apple offers it, you confirm the subscription, and Rendprop verifies it.
On screen: Sign in with Apple • Choose your business and role • Check Apple’s offer before confirming

04. Start in Listings
Open Listings and add the property before making media. The listing is the folder for its original photos, saved edits, videos, website, contact choice, and buyer enquiries.
On screen: Tap Listings • Add a home • Return to this listing for every later step

BUILD THE HOME

05. Enter the real address
Enter the home address carefully. Confirm the map suggestion when it is right, then add only facts you know, such as beds, baths, and square feet. An address can be edited before publishing.
On screen: Enter and confirm the address • Add known property facts • Review before saving

06. Choose who buyers contact
Open Who should buyers contact? Use your own contact card or select the client’s details when appropriate. Check the visible name, email, and phone before publishing so leads reach the right person.
On screen: Open buyer contact • Choose the listing’s contact card • Verify every public detail

07. Plan a simple photo route
Start outside if you have a clear front-of-home photo. Then photograph entry, living area, kitchen, bedrooms, baths, and the best outdoor spaces. A short, deliberate set is easier to review than many near-duplicates.
On screen: Front exterior, then key rooms • One clear view per useful angle • Keep extras for the gallery

08. Record a walkthrough only if useful
Drone-style walkthrough is optional. You can record in the app or upload an existing clip. Move slowly and smoothly, keep the phone level, and avoid quick turns. The photo-based listing video is a separate feature.
On screen: Optional: record or upload • Move steadily through connected rooms • Photo video remains independent

PHOTOS & AI

09. Add the original photos
In the listing toolbox, open Photos. Tap Add photos or Take a photo. Import keeps the original source, so you can compare every later version and return to the untouched image.
On screen: Listing toolbox → Photos • Add photos or Take a photo • Originals remain available

10. Capture for a clean result
Use natural light, hold the phone level, and shoot landscape for the website and listing video. Use the point-five lens only when the room needs it. Avoid cutting off walls or important fixtures.
On screen: Landscape and level • Fit the whole room • Retake blur or severe distortion

11. Open Photo versions
Tap a photo to inspect its saved versions. Switch between Original, Latest, Decluttered, and Staged when available. The selected listing version is a choice; creating an edit does not silently replace every photo.
On screen: Tap an image • Compare saved versions • Choose the version used on the listing

12. Use AI Photo Studio selectively
From an original, tap the wand or Edit originals with AI. Choose declutter, staging, sky, or another available edit. Check the credit or plan allowance shown before you start; each selected photo may use AI processing.
On screen: Start from Original • Pick an edit and photos • Review displayed usage before Generate

13. Review edits for truthfulness
Open the result beside its source. Check windows, doors, walls, fixtures, and room proportions. A staged room should not invent permanent features. Save only edits you would feel comfortable showing a buyer.
On screen: Compare with source • Check permanent details • Save or reject the result

14. Set the gallery and cover
Tap a photo’s listing control to use that saved version, or use Select All within a filtered group when the whole set is right. Choose a strong cover and review gallery order before publishing.
On screen: Use on listing, or Select All • Choose the cover photo • Review the final gallery order

15. Export the right photo copies
Photos can be shared as originals or prepared copies. Export photos offers organized versions and destination choices. Check your MLS image rules before sending files; website copies are not a substitute for the retained originals.
On screen: Photos → Share or Export • Choose destination and versions • Keep original files available

MAKE A VIDEO

16. Open Make a video
Back in the toolbox, tap Make a video. This uses your selected photos to create a downloadable listing or social video. It does not require a recorded walkthrough. Start with only the strongest reviewed images.
On screen: Toolbox → Make a video • Choose reviewed photos in order • Keep the first shot purposeful

17. Choose the photos in order
On Choose your photos, tap images in the sequence viewers should see. The numbered tiles show the exact saved versions. Change filters if you need originals or finished edits. Nothing is generated on this screen.
On screen: Tap 1, 2, 3 in viewing order • Use the saved version you reviewed • Continue to Choose the look

18. Set the screen shape
Choose the look next. Wide makes a landscape listing video; Tall makes a vertical social video. Pick the destination before checking crop, because the same photo fits differently in each shape.
On screen: Wide → landscape listing • Tall → vertical social • Preview the crop for that shape

19. Tune motion and timing
In Photo motion, Automatic varies the direction from photo to photo using the four twelve-percent movements. Or choose Push in, Pull back, Glide left, or Glide right to use one direction throughout. Set each photo's length: a simple entrance can be brief, while key rooms can hold longer. Then preview framing.
On screen: Photo motion → Automatic varies each photo • Or choose one direction • Set seconds → Preview framing

20. Check the framing review
Framing review shows the start and end crop. Watch for trimmed ceilings, doorways, or important details. If a photo does not fit the format, change the photo or shape before you spend usage on the video.
On screen: Compare start and end frames • Keep important features visible • Change photos if the crop fails

21. Add sound only when it helps
On the review screen, open Voice and music. Music, narration, and large words are optional. Preview sound, then pause it before continuing if needed. A clean silent video is valid for many listing uses.
On screen: Review → Voice & music • Preview and choose options • Leave them off for a silent export

22. Create, leave, and return
Review the selected photos, timing, and any credit quote. Tap Make my video once. Processing may take several minutes; you can leave the screen. Activity keeps the job under the right property.
On screen: Confirm the review • Tap Make my video once • Activity → Active tracks progress

23. Watch before you use it
When Activity says Ready, open the finished video. Check smoothness, crop, scene order, and sound. Save to Photos or Share for MLS or social delivery. Tap Use on listing website only after you approve the full video.
On screen: Activity → Ready → video • Watch the complete result • Use on listing website, Save, or Share

OPTIONAL VIDEO TOOLS

24. Aerial intro starts outside
Aerial intro is an optional AI opening shot, not captured drone footage. Use an original photo showing the whole front and actual roof. Review the crop, processing terms, and credit quote before making the opening.
On screen: Choose a true front exterior • Keep roof and entrance in frame • Review terms and usage

25. Accept an opening only if it works
Watch the saved aerial clip and check motion, roof shape, and the path toward the entrance. Use as the opening only if you accept it. When selected, it plays first; the following photo order stays intact.
On screen: Watch the saved opening • Check roof and smoothness • Use as opening → first clip

26. Tag a recorded walkthrough
For a separate recorded walkthrough, tag rooms at the moments they appear. These become tap-to-jump chapters on the property experience. Re-render refreshes that recording; it does not replace the photo-based listing video.
On screen: Open Drone-style walkthrough • Tag room transitions • Review the recorded result

PUBLISH THE WEBSITE

27. Choose the website design
Open Website design from the listing. Classic is the included design with paid access. Signature Estate and Coastal Luxe are optional property-specific upgrades when available. Preview a sample without publishing your draft.
On screen: Listing → Website design • Preview Classic or luxury samples • Confirm any upgrade before purchase

28. Review before publishing
Check the gallery cover, approved listing video, property facts, address, and buyer contact. The website uses the video you chose as its opening hero. If you accepted an aerial opening and included it in that video, it plays first on the website too. A recorded walkthrough remains separate.
On screen: Gallery and cover • Selected hero video · accepted aerial first • Facts and buyer contact

29. Publish, then share the link
Tap Publish listing when the review is complete. Your address-based share link includes a unique publication identifier, so a later agent can publish the same address without taking your page. Use Copy, QR, or Share your link.
On screen: Publish listing after review • Copy, QR, or Share • Buyers open it without the app

30. Use the right MLS link
The listing also offers an unbranded MLS link. Check your local MLS rules before using it. Keep the agent-branded share link for your own marketing, and confirm the published page on a browser before sending it broadly.
On screen: Agent link → direct marketing • MLS link → unbranded • Check rules and live page

FOLLOW UP & MANAGE

31. Leads stay with the property
Open Leads to see enquiries grouped by listing. Follow up from the actual buyer request, and verify the contact method you chose. A listing's website can collect enquiries while you work elsewhere in the app.
On screen: Open Leads • Filter by listing • Respond to each enquiry

32. Activity is the job dashboard
Activity groups work as Active, Ready, Needs attention, and History. Search for a property when you have many jobs. Open a row to return to its listing. When completed work can be counted, the app shows a percentage; otherwise it says Progress not reported instead of guessing.
On screen: Active → in progress • Ready or Needs attention → review • History → older work

33. Recover without starting over
If a job needs attention, open its listing and read the specific status before retrying. Do not repeatedly tap Create or buy credits again. Saved photos and requests remain attached to the home; use the offered recovery control.
On screen: Open the status • Use the specific Retry or Review action • Keep saved originals and edits

ACCOUNT & USAGE

34. Account holds your tools
Open Account for Profile and contact card, then Settings and plan. Settings includes your app walkthrough, team management when available, account data, and support paths. Ask AI can explain the screen you are on.
On screen: Account → Profile & contact card • Account → Settings & plan • Ask AI → contextual help

35. Understand the trial and plans
Pro, Max, Ultra, and Team are monthly plans. Check Apple's current price, included access, and any eligible seven-day offer before confirming. The limited trial supports one listing and limited photo edits; video and website publishing need paid access. If paid access is missing, select the original workspace and tap Restore purchases in Settings before buying again.
On screen: Plans → compare current offers • Apple shows exact renewal terms • Missing access → Restore purchases

36. Credits are separate from the plan
Credits and extra usage shows the shared workspace balance, monthly and purchased portions, and available top-ups. Purchased credits do not expire. The app shows a job's credit cost when spending is available. If a top-up is missing, tap Check pending purchase before buying again.
On screen: Settings → Credits & extra usage • Review available and held credits • Missing top-up → Check pending purchase

37. Work with a team carefully
Team workspaces share listing work and one credit balance. Owners or admins manage eligible purchases and invitations; seat availability depends on the current subscription. Check which workspace is selected before buying or editing a shared property.
On screen: Settings → Team • Confirm the selected workspace • Owner or admin manages purchases

38. One last review, then share
That is the practical path: accurate home details, clean originals, reviewed edits, a video you have watched, the right contact card, and a published website. Floor plans and three-dimensional capture are later features, not part of this first release.
On screen: Review photos and video • Publish and open the live link • Return to Leads and Activity

Prices, trial eligibility, available credits, and AI processing access are shown by the app and Apple at the time of use. This guide does not start a purchase or guarantee an offer.
